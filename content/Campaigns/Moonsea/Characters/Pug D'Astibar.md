# Pug D'Astibar

A wizard who worked at [[Mantor's Library]] under [[Kastra Brandywine]]. Stayed behind in [[Phlan]] near [[Denlor's Tower]] on personal business and keeps in touch with the party through [[Iltiza Ward]] via Sending. Currently level 7.

**Status:** In Phlan\
**First appears:** [[Chapter 11 - Episode 1 - Session 1 The Silver Pyramid]]

## Side quest: escort to Myth Drannor

Pug's "personal business" turned out to involve [[Elminster]] and [[Shadowdale]]. Secreted away from his clan, he travelled the Iron Road and on to Shadowdale, where Elminster and [[Sylune]] equipped him with the [[Silver Brooch]] and [[Crystal Vials]] and sent him, escorted by a separate group, toward [[Myth Drannor]] to collect sacred waters from an old elven shrine. He summoned his owl familiar, [[Nick Timoney]], to scout ahead through [[Cormanthor]]. During this trip he cast Counterspell to shut down a were-boar shaman's lightning spell, and landed a heavy Chromatic Orb that bounced between two targets for a combined 87 lightning damage.

**First appears (this thread):** [[Chapter 11 - Pug's Side Quest]]
