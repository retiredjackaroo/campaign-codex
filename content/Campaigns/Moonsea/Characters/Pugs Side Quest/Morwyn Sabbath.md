# Morwyn Sabbath

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Morwyn Sabbath.png]]

A High Elf College of Lore Bard, played by [[Jaag Nixon]]'s player as a secondary side quest character for [[Chapter 11 - Pugs Side Quest|Pug's Side Quest]]. Not to be confused with Jaag Nixon (the same player's main Moonsea character) or Derfel (the same player's Dark Sun character); the three are entirely separate people and should never be linked or merged.

Morwyn was never suited to Semberholme's quieter rhythms. He left young, not out of any grievance, simply because the old lore and half-remembered songs of his kin felt like a beginning rather than an ending, and he wanted to see how much further they went. He spent the better part of two decades on the road: taverns in the Dales, half-ruined shrines along the Moonsea, a season or two in Waterdeep when the coin ran thin, picking up stories, songs and the odd genuinely useful piece of magic along the way. Some of it he learned from proper tutors. A fair amount he picked up from people who probably shouldn't have been teaching anyone anything.

By now he's good at what he does, and he knows it. Not arrogant so much as simply unbothered by doubt; he's talked his way out of worse than a suspicious escort party before. He took this job partly for the coin, partly because [[Myth Drannor]] is precisely the sort of place that produces excellent material, and partly because a wizard from Phlan with Herald backing sounded like the start of a good story, and he's rarely been able to resist one of those.

The Knights, Sylvan Guard and outpost elves have their own long history together, which doesn't trouble him particularly. He's used to being the newcomer in a room, and he generally assumes he'll be a welcome addition once people get used to him, an assumption that's been right often enough that he sees no reason to doubt it now.

**Status:** Alive
