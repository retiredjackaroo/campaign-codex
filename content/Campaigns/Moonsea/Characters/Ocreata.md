# Ocreata

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Ocreata.png]]

A Drow Fighter for the Moonsea campaign, played by the same player as [[Keithelas Ward|Keithalas]]. Ocreata uses he/him pronouns and is named after a deadly mushroom. He is a distinct character and must not be merged with Keithalas/Keithelas Ward, [[Kjeorn]], or Salt.

Ocreata came to [[Myth Drannor]] as a child refugee and remembers its streets alive with people, songs and laughter. When the city fell, he ran instead of defending it. Myth Drannor has held him to that failure for roughly seven centuries: he cannot leave, and when he dies among the ruins he eventually awakens where he fell, never knowing how much time has passed.

He believes the city is seeking someone worthy to carry its memory and is testing [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] in particular. By helping Pug, Ocreata hopes to repay his debt and finally be free—or at least allowed to rest.

Ocreata wears ancient, torn armour and fights with a glaive. Long survival in the ruins has made him wary of spellcasting, which can produce unpredictable and explosive effects in the city's damaged magic.

**Status:** Alive; trapped in Myth Drannor and helping Pug's escort seek the city's judgment
**First appears:** [[Session 6 - Beneath the Same Moon]]

## Session 6, Beneath the Same Moon

- Found Pug, Morwyn, Ripper and Mar unconscious after Kjeorn's bargain and helped Herschel establish that they were alive.
- Tried to stop Herschel casting healing magic, fearing another magical accident; accepted mundane potions and a healer's kit.
- Led the survivors to a safe ruin, kept watch during their rest, then guided them around the Garden of Long Memory and along [[Coronals Way|Coronal's Way]].
- Revealed that the city has repeatedly restored him after death and will not allow him to leave until his debt is paid.
- Wept at [[Over the Hills and Far Away|Morwyn's song for Kjeorn]], the first music he had heard in Myth Drannor for more than seven hundred years.
- Warned the group about the [[Moon Deva]], stepped in front of Herschel and Morwyn, then charged it with his glaive. Four attacks were deflected, but repeated grazing strikes drew blood before the deva hurled him twenty feet.

## Session 7, The City Must Judge

- Fitted the third-quarter lunar disc opposite Mar's first-quarter disc.
- Recalled that Myth Drannor judges candidates through repeated trials of reverence for its history and memory, often in patterns of three. Kjeorn's sacrifice may count, but the judgment remains incomplete.
- Explained that the Moon Deva is a manifestation of the damaged but living Mythal and does not itself decide who is worthy.
- Tore Mar free from bindings of liquid moonlight and distracted the deva with glaive and javelin attacks while the others searched.
- Warned against forcing entry: Myth Drannor judged him unworthy and has trapped him through repeated deaths for roughly seven centuries.

## Session 8, Keeper of Memory

- Recognised the [[Heartstone of the Mythal]] as the place where Myth Drannor's fall began, which he has avoided for seven centuries out of shame. Went anyway: “I don't want to do that, but I will do that.”
- Translated the observatory's Espruar inscriptions, lit the torch for the [[Polished Black Stone]] experiment, and boosted Pug up to the broken gallery and caught him on the way down.
- Sheathed his glaive and knelt before the Heartstone: “I've sworn an oath before. I've let you and this city down. And I know my words mean little at this point, but know that I will do whatever it takes to keep you safe, keep the city safe, and see that the memories here live on.” He realised the city's bond with its Mythal had been forgotten through hubris.
- Recalled the [[Collapsed Crystal Conservatory]] as one of Tel'Mythar's most beloved institutions, and objected to removing its purifying vessel.
- Came to understand what Myth Drannor lost before its fall: nobles and archmages pursuing their own power, and Knights so sure of victory that the people were left unprotected.
- Volunteered to embody the virtue of Return, “because I'm stuck here.”
