# Ancient Seeds

A handful of seeds preserved in tiny crystal containers in a stone cabinet at the [[Collapsed Crystal Conservatory]]. They look cool, intact and healthy, but [[Herschel Blackroot]] could neither confirm they are viable nor identify a single species. Nothing growing in the conservatory now matches them.

**Status:** Carried by Herschel, who intends to plant them somewhere in future.
**First appears:** [[Session 8 - Keeper of Memory]]
