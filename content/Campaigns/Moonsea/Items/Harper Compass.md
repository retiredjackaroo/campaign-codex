# Harper Compass

A small compass recovered from the [[Harpers|Harper]] cache at the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]]. Its needle trembles and does not point north; it is a direction finder, and it points straight at [[Myth Drannor]] and its [[Myth Drannor|Mythal]]. [[Pug DAstibar|Pug]] identified it as a divination item (whoever bore it was almost certainly a Harper, or found it) and took it as the party's navigation aid for the last leg to the city.

Given the warning in [[Aurelien Voxs Journal]] that the ruins ignore maps and "returns", a magical pointer that fixes on the Mythal itself may prove more reliable than any map the party could draw.

**Held by:** [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]]
**First appears:** [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative]]

The compass successfully guided the escort through [[The Starwood]] to [[The Moon Arch]]. Its useful direction after entering [[Myth Drannor]] was not established before the ankheg attack.
