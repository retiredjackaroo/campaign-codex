# Silver Brooch

Given to [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] by [[Saloon]] in [[Elminster]]'s tower in [[Shadowdale]]. Insubstantial until Pug took hold of it, at which point it became solid. Intended to signal friendly intent to anyone who recognises it: "When they see this, they will know you arrive as a friend before a stranger." Used by placing a hand on the brooch and asking politely for aid.

**First appears:** [[Session 1 - Escort to Myth Drannor - Narrative]]
