# Potion of Heroism

A single potion recovered from the [[Harpers|Harper]] cache at the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]], alongside ten potions of healing. [[Pug DAstibar|Pug]] identified it with his alchemy tools: it grants 10 temporary hit points and the effect of a Bless spell for its duration.

After the potions were distributed round the group, [[Kjeorn]] took the Potion of Heroism, on the reasoning that the Bless suits a paladin.

**Held by:** [[Kjeorn]]
**First appears:** [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative]]
