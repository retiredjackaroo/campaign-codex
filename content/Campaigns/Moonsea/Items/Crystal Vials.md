# Crystal Vials

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Crystal Vials.png]]

Six crystal vials in a blue velvet case with silver runes, given to [[Pug D'Astibar]] by [[Elminster]] to collect sacred waters from the pool at the old elven shrine near [[Myth Drannor]]. [[Elminster]] would not allow Pug to touch the water directly; it must not touch skin.

**First appears:** [[Chapter 11 - Pug's Side Quest]]
