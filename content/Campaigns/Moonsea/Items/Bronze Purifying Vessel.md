# Bronze Purifying Vessel

A bronze vessel with a spout, hidden beneath a crystal pane where the river water enters the [[Collapsed Crystal Conservatory]]. River water flows in and leaves pristine. With it removed, the conservatory's channels carry ordinary river water, though the gardens did not visibly suffer at once.

**Status:** Taken by [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]], who plans to return it if no better use is found. The [[Heartstone of the Mythal]] and the Pool of Far Seeing were suggested as possibilities.
**First appears:** [[Session 8 - Keeper of Memory]]
