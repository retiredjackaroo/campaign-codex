# Aurelien Vox's Journal

A canvas-wrapped book found in the [[Harpers|Harper]] cache at the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]], written by the Harper operative [[Aurelien Vox]]. The handwriting starts steady and professional and degrades into something hard-pressed and shaky by the end.

The entries chart Vox's party being unsettled by [[Cormanthor]] and the ruins of [[Myth Drannor]]: a forest that "does not seem to require men at all," trails that soften as though they were never walked, ruins that disagree with the maps, voices in the stone, and party members seeing the same thing at different times. The final entries record the loss of a companion, **Aranis**, beneath the temple of [[Sehanine Moonbow]], and end with a warning: _do not trust your maps, do not trust your returns_, and "it is building a memory of us that we do not survive." The last page is blank, neither torn nor burned.

[[Pug DAstibar|Pug]] read the early and final entries, then deliberately left the journal in the cache (a Harper drop site is meant to stay stocked), treating its warning about navigation as a live hazard for the party's approach to the city.

**Location:** Left in the cache at the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]]
**First appears:** [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative]]
