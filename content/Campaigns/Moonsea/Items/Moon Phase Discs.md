# Moon Phase Discs

Polished stone discs made to fit nine sockets around the fountain in the [[Moon Plaza]]. One full-moon disc was already fitted when Pug's escort arrived. [[Herschel Blackroot]] found a black new-moon disc among the rubble and fitted it opposite the full moon, causing the hovering [[Moonstone]] to pulse.

Herschel's fey hawk identified two further likely disc locations before the [[Moon Deva]] destroyed it. The remaining discs have not been recovered, and the deva has enclosed the mechanism beneath a Wall of Force.

**Status:** Full moon and new moon fitted; seven sockets remain empty
**First appears:** [[Session 6 - Beneath the Same Moon]]
