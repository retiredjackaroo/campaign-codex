# Polished Black Stone

A perfectly smooth, non-reflective disc of polished black stone found lying loose on the platform of [[The Shattered Observatory]]. Looked into, it seems to deepen into a starscape, showing the skies over Myth Drannor and Cormanthor as they were at another time, moving at the stars' real pace. It grows warm when handled.

When [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] held it, its constellations flared with their engraved counterparts on the observatory platform until only the Moonboat remained. A silver line from the Moonboat's brightest star led to the [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]], and a pool of light formed beneath it.

**Status:** Last held by Pug; whether he kept it was not stated.
**First appears:** [[Session 8 - Keeper of Memory]]
