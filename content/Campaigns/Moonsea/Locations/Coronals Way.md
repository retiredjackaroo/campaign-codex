# Coronals Way

A broad ruined road through [[Myth Drannor]], used by Pug's escort to approach the [[Moon Plaza]] while avoiding the [[Grand Avenue]] and [[Garden of Long Memory]].

One stretch is broken by a vast crater that still smoulders and reeks of brimstone. [[Morwyn Sabbath]] connected it to the legend of three fiends released from a pocket dimension after the third Coronal's crowning and falling upon the city like burning meteors. The crater's centre still burns despite the seven centuries since Myth Drannor's fall.

**First appears:** [[Session 6 - Beneath the Same Moon]]
