# Garden of Long Memory

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Garden of Long Memory.png]]

An overgrown formal garden between the [[Grand Avenue]] and [[Hall of Scrolls]]. Crooked wrought-iron gates open onto cracked marble paths, wild hedges, moon-white statues and reflecting pools of impossibly clear water. Despite centuries of neglect, its fountains continue to flow and the garden retains a profound, melancholy elegance.

Hundreds of memorial stones stand among the flowers. Some are bound in rusted chains; others bear deep grooves where iron has worn across the stone. The garden seems peaceful, but its birds sound strangely distant, its air hangs unnaturally still and movement flickers at the edge of sight along otherwise empty paths.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Garden Memory.png]]

The garden showed the escort a living memory of families gathering for a hopeful citywide celebration. After [[Kjeorn]] broke a chain from one memorial, [[The Binder of Long Memory]] emerged with five captive spirits and four elemental mephits.

The ensuing battle ended when Kjeorn traded his own life to the Binder for the lives of the four fallen escorts. Pug, Ripper, Mar and Morwyn will awaken here, while the Binder has departed with Kjeorn's shade.
