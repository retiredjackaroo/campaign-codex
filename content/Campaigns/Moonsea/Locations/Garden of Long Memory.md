# Garden of Long Memory

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Garden Memory.png]]

An overgrown formal garden between the [[Grand Avenue]] and [[Hall of Scrolls]]. Its clear reflecting pool and persistent fountains stand among hundreds of memorial stones. Some markers are bound in rusted chains; others bear deep grooves where iron has worn across the stone.

The garden showed the escort a living memory of families gathering for a hopeful citywide celebration. After [[Kjeorn]] broke a chain from one memorial, [[The Binder of Long Memory]] emerged with five captive spirits and four elemental mephits.

**First appears:** [[Session 3 - Enter as Guests, Leave as Guardians]]
