# The Great Way Circle

A great teleportation circle in the [[High Mage Quarter]] of [[Myth Drannor]], ringed by the broken arches and pillars of a colonnade. Seven concentric rings of engraved moonstone spread from its centre, each standing for a principle the High Mages held necessary before journeying beyond one's home: **Curiosity, Humility, Wisdom, Fellowship, Responsibility, Trust and Return**. Most of its runes describe not where it leads, but how one should travel.

Infernal fire split the outer rings when the city fell; the inner rings are almost intact. A blue-white glow lingers beneath surviving glyphs, and every few minutes a few runes flicker in an activation sequence that never repeats and never completes. Two discs in the circle bear the sigils of Semberholme and Tangled Trees, the other cities of Cormanthyr.

## Session 8, Keeper of Memory

- Pug and Herschel worked out its purpose and principles. Repairing it would take more than one mage and a great deal of time, with no certainty of stability.
- Pug took one stone from the circle as a spell component. See [[Great Way Circle Stone]].
