# Collapsed Crystal Conservatory

The ruin of a vast crystal-roofed hall in the [[High Mage Quarter]] of [[Myth Drannor]]. Arches of white stone still trace its vaulted outline, enormous crystal panes lie broken on the floor or hang cracked in their frames, and nature has quietly reclaimed the gardens beneath.

It was one of Tel'Mythar's most beloved institutions: a living archive of magical plants gathered from across Toril and beyond, each documented and preserved. Every apprentice tended its gardens, whatever their magical ability, and scholars came here to think. The [[Heartstone of the Mythal]] still nourishes its soil.

Channels in the floor carry clear water diverted from the river. A central grove holds ancient trees, one with chiming silver leaves, and a kneeling stone inscribed _To tend one life well is to honour all life._ A sheltered vine still blooms with living silver moonflowers. None of the plants here compete with one another.
