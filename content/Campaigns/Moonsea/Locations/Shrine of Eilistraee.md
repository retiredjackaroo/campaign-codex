# Shrine of Eilistraee

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Characters/Pugs Side Quest/Sanctuary.png]]

An ancient shrine to [[Eilistraee]] deep in [[Cormanthor]], within the perimeter the escort normally guards but had never themselves entered. The party reached it at nightfall on the road to [[Myth Drannor]], about half a day short of the city.

The grove is a near-perfect circle roughly 100 feet across where the forest "has chosen not to grow." At its centre lies a broad reflecting pool, still as glass, ringed by worn stone benches, moss-covered columns (some fallen), and flowering vines. Moonlight pools here even before full dark. At the northern edge stands a marble arch, its keystone carved with a crescent moon and scenes of welcome: dancers, extended hands, a sword laid down. Its inscription, in the ancient Espruar dialect, reads: _"No soul who seeks the moon's mercy shall be turned away."_

Historically the elves of Myth Drannor used the shrine as a meeting place for those seeking refuge, especially drow escaping the Underdark. The place feels waiting rather than abandoned. Nothing living comes here: no tracks, no birdsong, no animal sign at all, and the plants confirm it is left utterly alone.

## The Harper Cache

Beneath a misaligned stone bench (its moss too uniform, roots deliberately routed around it) the party found a rotating mechanism concealing a [[Harpers|Harper]] drop site, marked with the crescent harp, leaf-wrapped dagger and watching eye. Inside a chest were waxed-vellum field notes, 10 healing potions and a [[Potion of Heroism]], a silver Harper pin, an inkwell, the [[Harper Compass]], and [[Aurelien Voxs Journal]]. Pug took the compass and, correctly, left the journal and notes so the cache would stay stocked.

## The Vision

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Characters/Pugs Side Quest/The grove of mercy.png]]

That night the reflecting pool rippled inward and the grove filled with translucent spirits: drow refugees welcomed by surface elves and centaurs, sharing bread and cloaks, a warrior laying down his bow, a choir of both peoples singing in the old dialect, a drow child dancing with a moon elf. The overwhelming impression was of hope and a "sacred mercy" that persisted here even through Myth Drannor's wars. The vision faded with the pool going still, leaving the party too moved to sleep at once. The choir's song is recorded at [[Hymn of the Moon's Welcome]].
