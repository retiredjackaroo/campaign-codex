# Heartstone of the Mythal

A pale moonstone at least thirty feet across, half sunk into the earth like the crown of a skull, at the bottom of the Crater of Opening in the [[High Mage Quarter]] of [[Myth Drannor]]. From it rises a crystalline tree of silver-white limbs that have grown out of the stone and twisted together in the shape of an old-growth tree. Glowing moonflowers bloom on its branches and drift down one by one; thousands lie on the marble around it, none withered.

It is the heart of the city's Mythal: what guarded Myth Drannor and kept it whole. It is gravely wounded. Fractures glow through the trunk, limbs have sheared away, and its roots, which spread beneath the whole city, are splintered or cleanly severed. Its light still pulses through the surviving roots like a slow, failing heartbeat. Near it, the city's wild magic ceases entirely.

The fiends imprisoned above the city came down here when Myth Drannor fell, and the first gates to their armies opened in this quarter. [[Ocreata]] has avoided the place for seven centuries out of shame.

## Session 8, Keeper of Memory

- Asked the escort, “Its keepers are gone. Are you here as conquerors, or to assume care?”
- Each companion answered: Pug offered his magic, Herschel searched it with spores, Ripper sought to understand it, Mar restored the river streams the crater had cut off, Ocreata knelt and renewed his oath, and Morwyn sang to comfort it.
- The spread of its cracks slowed and hundreds of new moonflowers bloomed. It is not restored, but was reminded that people worthy of its trust remain.
- It told them, “Stone falls, songs fade, and kingdoms end. But memory survives. Only if someone chooses to carry it,” and named [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] the Keeper of Memory.

**Status:** Dying slowly, but its decline has eased; water once again reaches it.
