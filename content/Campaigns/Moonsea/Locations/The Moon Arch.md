# The Moon Arch

The moonstone gateway into [[Myth Drannor]]. It tested visitors by memory, purpose and stewardship. Its outer inscription read, _Enter as guests, leave as guardians._ Inside: _Remember us kindly._

**First appears:** [[Session 3 - Enter as Guests, Leave as Guardians]]
