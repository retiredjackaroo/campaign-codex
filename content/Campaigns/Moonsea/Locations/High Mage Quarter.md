# High Mage Quarter

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/High Mage Quarter - Map.png]]

The northern district of [[Myth Drannor]], beyond the Noble Quarter, home to Tel'Mythar, the Academy of High Magic. It is the most devastated part of the city: almost nothing stands but the skeletal frames of buildings, the ground is broken and scorched, and in places embers still smoke. The devastation converges on the [[Heartstone of the Mythal]] at its centre, where the fiends' first gates opened when the city fell.

When [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] crossed into the quarter, it showed him its golden age: white towers and bridges, the Mythal shining at its heart, and students and masters of every people learning together. An old mage teaching children told him, “Magic was never the glory of Myth Drannor. We were its stewards.”
