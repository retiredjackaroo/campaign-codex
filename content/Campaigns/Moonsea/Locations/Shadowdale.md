# Shadowdale

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Shadowdale.webp]]

A dale watched over by the archmage [[Elminster]], who keeps a tower here. [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] arrived after a long, secretive journey from his clan, travelling the Iron Road and ferrying via two other towns before reaching Shadowdale, where Elminster and [[Sylune]] equipped him with the [[Silver Brooch]] and [[Crystal Vials]] and sent him on toward [[Myth Drannor]].

**First appears:** [[Session 1 - Escort to Myth Drannor - Narrative]]
