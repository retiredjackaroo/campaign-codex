# The Shattered Observatory

A ruined observatory on a rise in the [[High Mage Quarter]] of [[Myth Drannor]]. The ribs of its marble dome still arch against the open sky, staircases climb to galleries that no longer exist, and broken brass rings, crystal lenses and instruments litter the floor.

At its centre lies a platform carved from a single slab of moonstone and engraved with star charts. They are purely observational, with no divination: generations of scholars recording what was, for those who would come after. The platform is also a map of the sky as seen from Myth Drannor. A meridian pillar at its edge bears the Espruar inscription _The heavens need no witness. We watch not for their sake, but for our own._

## Session 8, Keeper of Memory

- Pug copied the star charts into his books.
- Herschel found the [[Polished Black Stone]], which shows the stars of another time. When Pug held it, only the Moonboat constellation remained lit; a silver line from its brightest star led to the [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]], and a pool of light formed beneath the temple under the words _The heavens mark the hour, the dead bear witness, and the waters receive the promise._
- Pug found the [[Observatory Apprentices Journal|apprentice's journal]] on the broken gallery.
