# The Starwood

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Starwood.png]]

The deep inner forest of [[Cormanthor]] approaching [[Myth Drannor]]. Its roads and bridges remain deliberately maintained. Animals here watched the escort without fear and appeared to herald [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]].
