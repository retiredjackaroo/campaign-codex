# Knights of Myth Drannor

Protectors of the [[Myth Drannor]] ruins, working alongside the Sylvan Guard and the outpost elves. [[Ripper]] was captured by the Knights after prying too deeply into the ruins' secrets, and now works alongside them, if reluctantly, keeping the deeper secrets of Myth Drannor safe and buried.

**First appears:** [[Chapter 11 - Pug's Side Quest]]
