# Harpers

A scattered, secretive network of spies, scouts and idealists who work from the shadows to oppose tyranny and the abuse of power, and to keep a balance between civilisation and the wilds. They prize individual freedom and the quiet trade of information over open force, operating in small cells rather than as an army.

**Party standing:** see [[Renown and Enmity]]
**First appears:** [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative]]

## The Cormanthor Drop Site

On Pug's side quest, the escort uncovered a Harper drop site concealed beneath a rotating stone bench at the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]] deep in [[Cormanthor]], marked with the order's crescent-harp, leaf-wrapped-dagger and watching-eye sigils. The chest held waxed-vellum field notes on the local trails and creatures, a silver harp-and-ivy pin, an inkwell, ten healing potions and a [[Potion of Heroism]], the [[Harper Compass]] that points at [[Myth Drannor]], and [[Aurelien Voxs Journal|the journal of Aurelien Vox]], a Harper operative lost in the ruins.

[[Pug DAstibar|Pug]], who has some affiliation with the Harpers, recognised the site for what it was: a cache meant for passing Harpers to use and replenish, and a place to leave word for others. He took only the compass and a share of the potions, and left the journal and field notes in place so the drop site would stay stocked.
