# Elminster

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Elminster.png]]

An old wizard who keeps a tower in [[Shadowdale]]. Met [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] there, along with [[Sylune]], plied him with cake and tea, and sent him on toward [[Myth Drannor]] with the [[Silver Brooch]] and the [[Crystal Vials]] in hand. Did not allow Pug to touch the vials' intended contents directly.

**Status:** At his tower in Shadowdale
**First appears:** [[Chapter 11 - Pugs Side Quest]]

## Realms Lore

Full name Elminster Aumar, known across Faerûn as the Sage of Shadowdale or simply the Old Mage. Born a shepherd boy in the long-destroyed realm of Athalantar, he lost his family and village to a tyrannical magelord as a young man, a loss that set him on the path to becoming a wizard in the first place. Centuries later he's one of the most powerful spellcasters alive, and the favoured Chosen of Mystra, the goddess of magic, which is a large part of why he hasn't aged out of existence yet.

He's spent most of that long life doing rather more than sitting in his tower. He's a close ally of the Harpers, has deep ties to the Knights of Myth Drannor, and keeps up a long friendship (among other things) with the Seven Sisters, of whom [[Sylune]] is one. Despite the reputation, he prefers subtlety over grand shows of force where he can manage it, though that doesn't stop him meddling in the affairs of Faerûn whenever he judges it necessary. He's usually recognisable by the hawk-like nose, the weathered face, the pipe, and a bluntness that tends to catch people off guard given how much power sits behind it.
