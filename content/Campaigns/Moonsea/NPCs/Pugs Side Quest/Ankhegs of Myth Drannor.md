# Ankhegs of Myth Drannor

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Ankhegs of Myth Drannor.png]]

Four burrowing monstrosities that erupted beneath the [[Grand Avenue]]. One dragged [[Kjeorn]] into a tunnel before [[Ripper]] opened its shell and [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] killed it.

In Session 4, Pug's Fireball killed [[Herschel Blackroot|Herschel]]'s captor. [[Mar]] tore [[Morwyn Sabbath|Morwyn]]'s captor from its tunnel and dropped it from the air, while Herschel repeatedly dragged the last determined ankheg away from its hole with Thorn Whip. Ripper killed the final combatant by driving a sword through its underbelly.

**First appears:** [[Session 3 - Enter as Guests, Leave as Guardians]]

**Resolved:** [[Session 4 - The Binder of Long Memory]]
