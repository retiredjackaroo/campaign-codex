# Ankhegs of Myth Drannor

Four burrowing monstrosities that erupted beneath the [[Grand Avenue]]. One dragged [[Kjeorn]] into a tunnel before [[Ripper]] opened its shell and [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] killed it. At the break, others held [[Herschel Blackroot]] and [[Morwyn Sabbath]].
