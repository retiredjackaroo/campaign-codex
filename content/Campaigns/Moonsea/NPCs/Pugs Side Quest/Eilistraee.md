# Eilistraee

The good-aligned drow goddess, and the deity to whom the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]] deep in [[Cormanthor]] is dedicated. Considered a lesser deity in the drow pantheon and a rare chaotic-good counterpoint to Lolth, she is the goddess of beauty, song, dance, freedom and moonlight, and is aligned in spirit with [[Sehanine Moonbow]]. One of her names is the **Lady of the Dance**, and she is commonly depicted as figures dancing beneath a full moon.

Her shrine in the forest was historically a place of refuge, where the elves of [[Myth Drannor]] welcomed those seeking sanctuary, especially drow escaping their underworld life. The vision the party witnessed there, drow refugees received with bread, cloaks and song, is an echo of that mercy.

**Status:** Venerated at the grove; historical / lore
**First appears:** [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative]]
