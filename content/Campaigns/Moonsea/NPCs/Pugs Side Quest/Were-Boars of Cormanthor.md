# Were-Boars of Cormanthor

A hunting party of were-boar lycanthropes bound to [[Malar]], the Beastlord, roaming the Midwood of [[Cormanthor]]. The escort came upon them mid-butchery over a slain stag and tried to slip past; the boars detected them and attacked, opening the fight that ran through the end of Session 1 and the first half of Session 2.

The pack fought in hybrid form, frenzying berserkers backed by a spellcasting **shaman** (Moonbeam, lightning, a clutch of teeth-and-bone totems used as spell focuses) and a **chief**, with a couple of giant boars alongside. In their human-passing forms they read as monstrosities rather than humanoids, a distinction that mattered when [[Herschel Blackroot]] checked whether he could animate one.

By the end of the fight several lay dead and reverted to human corpses in shredded clothing; the chief, the shaman and at least one other broke and fled into the forest. They know the escort is abroad in the wood. Had [[Ripper]] not escaped the smoke, the pack intended to drag her back to a shrine of Malar.

**Status:** Some slain (reverted to human), some fled
