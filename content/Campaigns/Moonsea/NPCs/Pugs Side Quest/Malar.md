# Malar

The Beastlord, a savage deity of hunters, predators and the bloodlust of the wild. The [[Were-Boars of Cormanthor]] that ambushed the escort in the Midwood are bound to him, and the GM later revealed that, had [[Ripper]] been captured, she would have been strapped to a shrine of Malar and made to choose between drinking the shaman's blood to become one of the pack or becoming their feast.

His presence stands in deliberate contrast to the mercy of the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]] nearby: predation and forced conversion on one side of the forest, sanctuary and welcome on the other.

**Status:** Cult presence in [[Cormanthor]]
**First appears:** [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative]]
