# Silvanus

The god of wild nature. [[Kjeorn]] described his oath as service to nature itself rather than to a particular god, but after he gave himself to [[The Binder of Long Memory]] to save the rest of the escort, Silvanus granted the new shade an enduring oak-leaf light. He did not overturn Kjeorn's freely chosen contract, destroy the devil or prevent physical suffering. His boon preserves something the Binder cannot reach: peace beneath the pain, sustained by the memory of wind in oak leaves, rain on earth, the turning seasons and the friends Kjeorn saved.

**First appears:** [[Session 5 - The Price of Memory]]
