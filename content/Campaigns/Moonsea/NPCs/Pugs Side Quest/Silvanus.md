# Silvanus

The god of wild nature. [[Kjeorn]] described his oath as service to nature itself rather than to a particular god, but after he gave himself to [[The Binder of Long Memory]] to save the rest of the escort, Silvanus granted the new shade an enduring oak-leaf light: not release from death or bondage, but mercy from pain in eternal service.

**First appears:** [[Session 5 - The Price of Memory]]
