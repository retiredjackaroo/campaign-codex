# Aurelien Vox

A [[Harpers|Harper]] operative, and the author of the journal recovered from the drop site at the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]]. His handwriting begins steady and professional and degrades as the journal goes on, charting his party's slow unravelling in [[Cormanthor]] as the forest and the ruins of [[Myth Drannor]] seemed to fold space and memory around them.

By the final entry the ink is pressed hard and shaky. He records that a companion, **Aranis**, went missing after they followed her into the ruins beneath the temple of [[Sehanine Moonbow]], where the stone steps had moved overnight. His last legible lines warn: do not trust your maps, do not trust your returns, and "it is building a memory of us that we do not survive." The rest of the page is blank, not torn or burned, just blank. His own fate is unrecorded, and presumed grim.

> [!note] Name uncertainty
> An earlier journal entry names a companion **"Ironness"** ("Ironness has assured me is nothing, but Ironness is wrong more often than she admits"), while the final entry names **Aranis** as the one lost beneath the temple. These may be the same person garbled by the speech-to-text, or two separate members of Vox's party. Left flagged rather than merged.

**Status:** Missing, presumed lost
**First appears:** [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative]]
