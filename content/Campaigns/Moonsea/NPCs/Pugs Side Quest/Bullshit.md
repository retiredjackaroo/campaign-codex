# Bullshit

![[Bullshit.png]]

*Bullshit greeting Kjeorn, as imagined and sketched by [[Morwyn Sabbath]].*

[[Kjeorn]]'s celestial steed, summoned in the form of an elk. [[Morwyn Sabbath]] tried and failed to enchant Bullshit into licking Kjeorn's face for eight hours. Bullshit later fought the ankhegs and tried to pull [[Herschel Blackroot]] free.

Bullshit fought beside Kjeorn against the Binder and its mephits, then was killed in the Garden of Long Memory before Kjeorn's final bargain.

**First appears:** [[Session 3 - Enter as Guests, Leave as Guardians]]
