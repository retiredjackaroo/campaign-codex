# Sylvanus

The god of wild nature, honoured by [[Kjeorn]] through his oath to nature and its ancient ways. When Kjeorn gave himself to [[The Binder of Long Memory]] to save the rest of the escort, Sylvanus could not prevent his death or bondage, but granted the new shade an enduring oak-leaf light: mercy from pain in eternal service.

**First appears:** [[Session 5 - The Price of Memory]]
