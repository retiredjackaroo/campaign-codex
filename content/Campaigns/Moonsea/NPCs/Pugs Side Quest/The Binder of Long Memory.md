# The Binder of Long Memory

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Binder of Long Memory.png]]

A towering chain devil haunting the [[Garden of Long Memory]], crowned in rusted shackles and surrounded by living black chains. Five spirits—men, women and children—are bound to him and can manifest effects on his command. Four elemental mephits accompanied him.

The Binder appeared after [[Kjeorn]] broke the chains around a memorial stone. He called the escort “warm ones” and claimed the garden as his own. The party suspects the spirits are captives and intends to free rather than harm them.

**Status:** Active in combat.
**First appears:** [[Session 4 - The Binder of Long Memory]]
