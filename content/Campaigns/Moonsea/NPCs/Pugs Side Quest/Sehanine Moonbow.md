# Sehanine Moonbow

The elvish goddess of the moon, and the deity held in highest honour in [[Myth Drannor]] during its heyday. Where the whole elvish pantheon (and gods of many other pantheons besides) kept shrines and clerics in the city at its height, Sehanine Moonbow was its foremost patron. She is aligned in spirit with [[Eilistraee]], whose forest [[Shrine of Eilistraee|shrine]] shares the moon's iconography.

Her name surfaced in the party's reading of [[Aurelien Voxs Journal|Aurelien Vox's journal]]: the missing Harper **Aranis** was last followed into the ruins beneath the temple of Sehanine Moonbow, where the stone steps "were not where they were yesterday."

**Status:** Historical / lore
