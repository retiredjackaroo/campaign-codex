# Sylune

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Sylune.png]]

A spectral, ghostlike woman encountered in [[Elminster]]'s tower in [[Shadowdale]]. One of the Seven Sisters, ghostlike since her death and closely tied to Elminster's household in Shadowdale. Identified as Sylune (Syl, for short) from the prep art for this session, titled "Tea with El and Syl", after initially being logged under the placeholder name "Saloon". Presented [[Pug D'Astibar]] with the [[Silver Brooch]], which became solid once he took it from her.

**Status:** Alive (as a spectral presence)
**First appears:** [[Chapter 11 - Pug's Side Quest]]

## Realms Lore

Full name Anastra Sylune Silverhand, eldest of the Seven Sisters, born (like all her sisters) as one of Mystra's own Chosen. Known in life and after as the Witch of Shadowdale, she loved the place fiercely and spent her long life protecting it, taking as much pleasure in small acts of kindness as in her considerable magecraft. She had little patience for anyone who used magic to bully or oppress others.

She died defending Shadowdale from dragon-riding raiders and, rather than moving on, stayed behind as a ghost, watching over the dale and its people much as she had in life. Only her closest friends and a handful of Harpers ever knew for certain that she'd lingered on, though plenty of the locals had their suspicions. Elminster in particular remained close to her long after her death, which fits neatly with the tea, cake, and easy familiarity Pug walked in on.
