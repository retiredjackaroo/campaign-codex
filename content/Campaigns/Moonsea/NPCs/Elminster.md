# Elminster

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Elminster.png]]

An old wizard who keeps a tower in [[Shadowdale]]. Met [[Pug D'Astibar]] there, along with [[Sylune]], plied him with cake and tea, and sent him on toward [[Myth Drannor]] with the [[Silver Brooch]] and the [[Crystal Vials]] in hand. Did not allow Pug to touch the vials' intended contents directly.

**Status:** At his tower in Shadowdale
**First appears:** [[Chapter 11 - Pug's Side Quest]]
