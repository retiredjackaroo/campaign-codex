# Sylune

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Sylune.png]]

A spectral, ghostlike woman encountered in [[Elminster]]'s tower in [[Shadowdale]]. Presented [[Pug D'Astibar]] with the [[Silver Brooch]], which became solid once he took it from her.

**Status:** Alive (as a spectral presence)
**First appears:** [[Chapter 11 - Pug's Side Quest]]

## Note for James

Last session I flagged this NPC under a placeholder name, "Saloon", almost certainly a mis-transcription. The Roll20 handout art from prep for this session is titled "Tea with El and Syl", which strongly suggests Syl, short for Sylune. Sylune fits well independently too: she's one of the Seven Sisters, ghostlike since her death, and closely tied to Elminster's household in Shadowdale in published Forgotten Realms lore. I've renamed the page on that basis, but it's still an inference from a handout title rather than something confirmed directly in your session notes, so flag it if that's wrong.
