# Hymn of the Moon's Welcome

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Characters/Pugs Side Quest/The grove of mercy.png]]

The song the choir of spirits sang during the vision at the [[Shrine of Eilistraee]], witnessed by the escort on Pug's side quest (see [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative|The Grove of Mercy]]). It rose, without instruments, from surface elves and drow singing together as drow refugees were welcomed into the grove.

The words are in the old Espruar of Cormanthyr, the common Elvish of [[Myth Drannor]]'s heyday. None of the party spoke it fluently, but fragments drifted through: _walk no longer in darkness, lay down your fear; the moon sees all children alike; here no blade pursues; here every dance begins again._ [[Morwyn Sabbath]] wanted the full text as the basis for his own songs.

> [!note] Translation
> The full text below is the original Espruar as provided by the GM. An English translation exists but, in the GM's words, loses something out of the original; it is still to be added here once supplied.

## Letha Seldarine Sehanine

> **I**
> Laya tel'quessir anarath.
> Laya tel'quessir ess'len.
> Uumae tel'ped en'aran.
> Uumae tel'dhaer en'vaela.
> Leth shalan en'naur.
> Vaer...
> Sehanine quel ussta.
>
> **II**
> Lina en'linath.
> Seldru tel'vael en'lath.
> Laa vae quess lir en'daur.
> Laa silar tel'dra en'quessir.
> Sae vaor tel'dur naur...
> Vae...
> Leth en'dor.
>
> **III**
> Ithilor tel'quessir.
> Tel'quor en'naela.
> Cormanth tel'istar vaela.
> Laa en'ped naur.
> Sii lath tel'dor.
> Vaer...
> En'Sehanine.
>
> **IV**
> Lina, tel'vaor.
> Lina, tel'quor.
> Lina, tel'daur.
> Sehanine laa tel'quor.
> Leth quel lir.
> Ithil.
> Leth tel'quor.
>
> **V**
> Riina, tel'linath.
> Toran, tel'cormanth.
> Quessir tel'naur.
> Quessir tel'ithil.
> Lina en'selu.
> Laa tel'aran.
> Laa tel'dra.
> Leth en'vaela.
>
> **VI**
> Sae vaor...
> Laa tel'dor.
> Sae quor...
> Lina.
> Sae naur quel.
> Quel ess'len.
> Quel ess
> Riina quel.
> Quel.
>
> **VII**
> Leth vaor quel.
> Anar quel.
> Vaor en'dor.
> Vaor en'Cormanth.
> Vaor en'linath.
> Quel lir.
> Laa quel ussta.
> Quel tel'dor.
> Sae lir quel.
> Leth vaor quel.
>
> **VIII**
> Sae tel'vaor...
> Quel ithil.
> Sae tel'quor...
> Quel ithil.
> Sae anar quel.
> Sae quessir tel'dor.
> Quel...
> Ithil laa quel tel'quessir.
> Ithil laa quel tel'dra.
>
> **IX**
> Sae vaor tel'lor...
> Quel Cormanth.
> Quel quessir.
> Quel lina tel'ess.
> Quel ussta.
> Quel Sehanine.
>
> **Final Refrain**
> Leth tel'quor.
> Leth tel'linath.
> Vaor tel'vaela.
> Lina tel'dor.
> Vaor en'dor.
> Sae vaor tel'lor...
> Quel Cormanth.
>
> Quel ithil.
