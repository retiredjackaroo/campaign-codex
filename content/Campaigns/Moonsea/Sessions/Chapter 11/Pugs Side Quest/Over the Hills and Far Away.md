_Morwyn Sabbath's song to honour Kjeorn, preserved in full as supplied._

He left the woodland of his birth,\
To find the wider, stranger earth;\
Too green to fear the road ahead,\
Over the hills and far away.

**Chorus**

Raise up a glass to Kjeorn’s name,\
And one to Bullshit just the same;\
We’ll laugh through tears and carry on,\
Over the hills and far away.

When darkness asked a soul to pay,\
To spare Pug, Ripper, Mar, Morwyn,\
He chose himself and set us free,\
Over the hills and far away.

The Binder then bound him in shade,\
Yet oak-leaf light will never fade;\
Silvanus guards his hidden light,\
Over the hills and far away.

Now every dawn and road we take\
Will bear the gift he chose to make;\
The forest keeps what none can take,\
Over the hills and far away.

Herschel squatted by the trees,\
With breeches gathered round his knees;\
The woodland beasts all fled that day,\
Over the hills and far away.
