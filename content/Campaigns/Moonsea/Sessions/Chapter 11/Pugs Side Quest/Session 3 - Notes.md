# Session 3 - Notes

_Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 3_

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Session 3 - End of Ankheg Battle.png]]

> [!caption] Where the action finished
> One ankheg is dead. Herschel and Morwyn remain grappled at separate tunnel mouths while the surviving ankhegs hold the avenue.

The escort crossed [[The Starwood]], passed the [[The Moon Arch|Moon Arch]] tests and entered [[Myth Drannor]]. The city showed them the restored [[Grand Avenue]] before four ankhegs erupted beneath it; the session ended mid-combat with [[Herschel Blackroot]] and [[Morwyn Sabbath]] grappled. See [[Session 3 - Enter as Guests, Leave as Guardians|the narrative]].

## Major Events

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Starwood.png]]

- Cormanthor deepened into the deliberately maintained Starwood.
- Animals heralded Pug while he carried the [[Silver Brooch]]; the cause is not confirmed.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Moon Arch Test.png]]

- The Moon Arch tested **memory**, **purpose** and **stewardship**.
- Pug offered the dream that committed him to saving [[Phlan]].
- Kjeorn declared his purpose to protect nature, Pug and Myth Drannor.
- Herschel entrusted his quarterstaff; the city returned it after entry.
- The arch read: **“Enter as guests, leave as guardians.”** Inside: **“Remember us kindly.”**

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The City of Song.png]]

- Pug recognised the [[Hall of Scrolls]], [[Garden of Long Memory]] and [[House of Song]].

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The First Memory.png]]

- The Grand Avenue briefly appeared restored and crowded for a celebration.
- Mar heard chains northeast before four [[Ankhegs of Myth Drannor|ankhegs]] erupted.

## Combat Contributions

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Fountain.png]]

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/What Dwells Below.png]]

- **Ripper:** transformed, controlled movement with her whip, cast Magic Weapon and opened Kjeorn's captor's shell.
- **Pug:** failed to Polymorph an ankheg but triggered a city-wide memory; killed Kjeorn's captor with lightning Chromatic Orb.
- **Morwyn:** cast Slow, used True Strike and Bardic Inspiration; ended grappled at a tunnel mouth.
- **Mar:** triggered birdsong and rampant growth with Barkskin; became a giant eagle and attacked from above.
- **Kjeorn:** used Shining Smite, survived being dragged into a tunnel and tried to free Herschel.
- **Bullshit:** attacked an ankheg and tried to pull Herschel free.
- **Herschel:** activated Symbiotic Entity and Shillelagh; badly wounded his captor while grappled.
- **Snotty:** survived the eruption and acid and attacked under Herschel's direction.

## End State

- Red ankheg dead in its tunnel.
- Kjeorn free, wounded and covered in ichor; Pug beside the corpse in the tunnel.
- Herschel grappled by badly wounded orange.
- Morwyn grappled by blue at the lip of its tunnel.
- Green alive; slowed earlier.
- Mar in giant-eagle form; Ripper's Magic Weapon active; Morwyn concentrating on Slow.
