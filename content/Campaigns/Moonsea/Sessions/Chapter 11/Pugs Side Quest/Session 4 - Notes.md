# Session 4 - Notes

*Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 4*

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Pugs Side Quest Thumbnail.png]]

The escort finished the ankheg battle, followed the sound of chains into the [[Garden of Long Memory]], witnessed another living memory of Myth Drannor and broke a chain from a memorial stone. That summoned [[The Binder of Long Memory]], and the session ended mid-combat. See [[Session 4 - The Binder of Long Memory|the narrative]].

## Present

[[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]], [[Kjeorn]], [[Herschel Blackroot]], [[Mar]], [[Morwyn Sabbath]], [[Ripper]], [[Bullshit]] and [[Snotty]].

## Major Events

- Pug's Fireball killed Herschel's captor and heavily burned another ankheg.
- Mar pulled Morwyn's captor from its tunnel and dropped both from thirty feet, freeing him.
- Herschel repeatedly used Thorn Whip to stop the last ankheg escaping with Kjeorn.
- Kjeorn escaped; Herschel nearly finished the creature; Ripper killed it from beneath.
- Kjeorn cast Prayer of Healing, restoring the party and granting a short rest.
- Pug saw a cracked rose-white moonstone and a flowering moonstone tree pulse beneath the city's memory echo.
- Ripper tracked chains into the Garden of Long Memory.
- The garden showed a memory of families gathering for a celebration involving the Coronal.
- Herschel partially read Eliana Starblue's memorial: “gardener and friend”; only *flower*, *song* and *crumble* survived from its poem.
- Kjeorn broke the chains around another memorial with a crowbar.
- The Binder appeared with five chained spirits and four elemental mephits.

## Combat Contributions

- **Pug:** Fireball ended one ankheg; Mind Sliver triggered the moonstone vision; broke free from the Binder's restraining vines using Heroic Inspiration.
- **Kjeorn:** survived being carried toward a tunnel, healed through the ankheg aftermath, broke the memorial chain, struck the Binder and fought beside Bullshit after wild magic teleported him into the reflecting pool.
- **Herschel:** controlled the ankhegs with Thorn Whip, nearly killed the last one with Shillelagh and Primal Strike, investigated the memorials, and confused one mephit.
- **Ripper:** prioritised rescues throughout the ankheg fight, killed the final creature, tracked the chains, frightened two mephits and engaged the Binder.
- **Mar:** lifted and dropped an ankheg to free Morwyn, blocked a tunnel, carried Morwyn above the garden, grappled an earth mephit and held it even after the Binder dragged her down.
- **Morwyn:** healed the party during the ankheg fight, supplied the aerial soundtrack, attacked the Binder with Dissonant Whispers and used Cutting Words to stop one chain strike against Mar.
- **Bullshit:** healed Kjeorn during the ankheg fight and attacked a mephit in the garden.
- **Snotty:** remained with Herschel and survived the ankheg battle; no distinct garden action was recorded before the break.

## End State

- The ankhegs are dead or driven away; the Grand Avenue is clear.
- Combat is live in the Garden of Long Memory.
- The Binder has five chained spirits; the escort suspects they can be freed.
- Four mephits are present; Mar holds one earth mephit and another is confused.
- Mar and Morwyn are prone after the Binder dragged them from the air.
- Pug is free of the restraining vines.

## Uncertainties

- “Eliana Starblue” is phonetically clear enough to record in Notes, but no entity page was created from one damaged memorial inscription.
- The cracked moonstone and flowering stone tree are recorded as a vision, not yet canonised as a named entity or confirmed location.
- The elementals were described inconsistently as earth, dust, fire and magma mephits; no separate entity pages were created.

## Next

- Defeat the Binder without harming the chained spirits, and determine whether they can be freed.
- Learn why memorial stones were chained and what breaking one changed.
- Investigate the cracked moonstone source beneath Myth Drannor.
- Continue toward the temple of [[Sehanine Moonbow]] and the pool of radiance.
