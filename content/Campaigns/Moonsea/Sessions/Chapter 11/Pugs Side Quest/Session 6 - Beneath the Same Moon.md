_Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 6_

## The Name on the Stone

[[Herschel Blackroot]] returned to the [[Garden of Long Memory]] with dirt on his robes, green on his cheeks and both arms full of moss, shell fungus and flowers. The distraction had lasted longer than intended. He expected to find his companions where he had left them. Instead, Pug, Morwyn, Ripper and Mar lay scattered across bloodied earth, and a scarred drow in ancient, torn armour knelt among their bodies with a hand at Ripper's throat.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Herschel Finds the Group.png]]

> [!caption] Mysterious circumstances
> Herschel hauled up his trousers and discovered that the afternoon had somehow become more awkward.

Herschel advanced with a spell ready. The stranger—[[Ocreata]]—sprang up and became far more interested in the flowers than in explaining himself. When Herschel tried to cast healing magic, Ocreata lunged for his hands in alarm. Magic in Myth Drannor, he insisted, could explode. Herschel slipped the clumsy restraint, but paused long enough to understand that Ocreata was afraid rather than murderous.

The companions were alive. Herschel used his healer's kit to stabilise the gravely wounded Ripper and Mar, then poured his two healing potions into Pug and [[Morwyn Sabbath]]. They woke battered and confused before a chained memorial stone bearing [[Kjeorn]]'s name. Kjeorn himself—and [[The Binder of Long Memory]]—were gone.

Morwyn understood first. Kjeorn had accepted the Binder's bargain in their place. Pug stared at the stone until the truth settled, then promised that once he escaped the city he would return to the library and find a way to bring Kjeorn back. Morwyn promised him a song.

## The Drow Who Could Not Leave

Ocreata had heard the battle and Morwyn's music, but arrived only after it was over. He had come to Myth Drannor as a child refugee while its streets still held people, songs and laughter. When the city fell, he ran instead of defending it. Seven centuries later, Myth Drannor still refused to release him.

He had died many times among the ruins. Each time he woke where he had fallen, with an unknowable span of time gone. The city would not let him rest and would not let him leave until he repaid a debt he could not clearly explain. He knew only that Myth Drannor wanted someone worthy to carry its memory, and that it seemed especially interested in Pug.

Ocreata led the exhausted party to shelter in a ruined building beyond the city's named districts. Ripper and Mar needed longer to recover, so they remained behind while Ocreata, Herschel, Morwyn and Pug kept watch and rested. Chains rattled in the distance all through the night, followed by laughter no one could locate.

At the next fire, Morwyn sang the song he had promised: [[Over the Hills and Far Away]], a road-song for Kjeorn, his sacrifice and the celestial stag called Bullshit. Ocreata wept. He had not heard singing in the city for more than seven hundred years. Morwyn then added a verse celebrating Herschel's equally memorable contribution to the woodland.

## The Road to the Moon

Kjeorn had bought their lives so that the mission could continue. The four set out for the [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]], leaving Ripper and Mar concealed with a note and taking the northern route around the garden. Pug looked longingly toward the [[Hall of Scrolls]], once the greatest store of learning in Faerûn, but Ocreata warned that its guardians still endured. A coin toss settled the matter: they would stay on mission.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Myth Drannor - Map.png]]

On [[Coronals Way|Coronal's Way]], they passed a vast crater that still smouldered and reeked of brimstone. Morwyn recalled the legend of three fiends imprisoned above Myth Drannor in a pocket dimension. Soon after the crowning of the city's third Coronal, prophecy broke their prison and the fiends fell upon the city like burning meteors. The scar in the road appeared to be one of their ancient impact sites, yet its centre still burned.

The road rose towards the temple. Even ruined, it stood like a pale mountain of stone and crystal, its bronze-bound doors still unbroken and its silver inlay showing the phases of the moon around a flowering tree. Statues of priests, pilgrims, families and children lined the approach. Above the entrance, Ocreata read the surviving Espruar:

> May every soul find its path beneath the same moon.

## The Coronal's Memory

At the heart of the [[Moon Plaza]] stood a shattered fountain. A person-sized [[Moonstone]] hovered above it, bright with the magic that had once helped sustain Myth Drannor's Mythal. Nine fitted sockets circled the fountain's base: one held a polished full-moon disc, while eight stood empty.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Pugs Memory - Coronal Day.png]]

The moment Pug crossed into the plaza, the ruins vanished from his sight. White marble shone beneath an afternoon sun. Water sang through the restored fountain. Thousands of elves filled the terraces and balconies while musicians played and children raced between the crowd. Twenty-four ceremonial knights emerged from the temple, followed by the translucent memories of the first two Coronals and the living third.

The new Coronal did not demand that the people kneel. He knelt before them.

> “The honour bestowed upon me is not dominion. It is service.”

As he spoke of the city's future, his gaze seemed to settle impossibly upon Pug: Myth Drannor entrusted itself to its people, and now to him. Every elf in the plaza placed a hand over their heart and answered together. Then the bells, music and sunlight died. Pug stood once more in the ruins, facing a crystal that had turned towards him.

## The Guardian's Test

The Moonstone answered peace with radiant lightning. Pug was struck first. Ocreata stepped between the crystal and his companions, warning that it was only the ward; the true guardian had not yet appeared. Morwyn attacked the crystal with a psychic shriek like an ancient dial-up modem. The stone showed no sign of caring.

Herschel saw the puzzle. Among the rubble he found a polished black [[Moon Phase Discs|new-moon disc]] and fitted it into the socket opposite the full moon. Light pulsed through the hovering crystal.

The air folded open before him. A vast [[Moon Deva]] appeared above the plaza, seated cross-legged in a storm of blue and violet light. It looked down at Herschel and slowly wagged one finger.

The party kept searching while crystal and guardian attacked. Pug countered the deva's Bigby's Hand before it could form. Morwyn struck a note and commanded the celestial to grovel; matching his pitch perfectly, it answered, “No.” Herschel summoned a fey hawk to circle the plaza. It marked two buried discs before ribbons of liquid moonlight bound its wings and dropped it from the air. Pug called [[Nick Timoney]] into the search, but the owl completed a beautiful arc with little backward rolls, found nothing at all, and looked back as though awaiting praise.

Radiant force swept the plaza. Ocreata, Morwyn and Herschel were burned; Ocreata then charged the deva with his glaive. The guardian turned aside every blow almost effortlessly, but Ocreata kept grazing its fingers until he drew a thin line of blood. One open palm sent him flying twenty feet.

The deva raised a dome of invisible force around the fountain and its lunar mechanism. At that moment, Ripper and Mar—finally recovered enough to follow the party's note—walked into the Moon Plaza with pounding headaches.

The battle was still underway.

## Loose Ends

- The Moon Deva and Moonstone remain active, and the deva has enclosed the fountain beneath a Wall of Force.
- One full-moon disc and one new-moon disc are fitted. Herschel's hawk identified two more possible discs before it was destroyed.
- Ripper and Mar have reached the plaza; the whole surviving escort is now present again.
- Ocreata believes helping Pug prove worthy to carry Myth Drannor's memory may finally pay his debt and free him.
- Pug intends to find a way to recover Kjeorn from the Binder.
- The six [[Crystal Vials]] remain empty, and the pool beneath Myth Drannor is still unreached.
