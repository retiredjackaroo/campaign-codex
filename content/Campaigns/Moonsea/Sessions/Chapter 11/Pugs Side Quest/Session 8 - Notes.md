_Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 8_

The escort reactivated the Moonstone's ward, rested at the edge of the Garden of Long Memory and travelled north into the ruined High Mage Quarter. Pug saw the quarter alive again and received three guiding lines from an old mage. At the Shattered Observatory a star stone showed the pool of radiance beneath the Temple of Sehanine Moonbow. At the Heartstone of the Mythal the whole escort answered its question, slowing its decline, and Pug accepted the title Keeper of Memory. The GM confirmed afterwards that the quarter's main test is complete. See [[Session 8 - Keeper of Memory|the narrative]].

## Present

- Active: [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]], [[Herschel Blackroot]], [[Mar]], [[Morwyn Sabbath]], [[Ocreata]] and [[Ripper]].
- Ocreata's player joined partway through, after Pug's vision of the living quarter; Ocreata is treated as present throughout.
- No combat this session.

## Before Leaving the Moon Plaza

- One lunar disc was removed from the fountain (by Ocreata, per the table), reactivating the [[Moonstone]] to guard the [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]] while the escort is away. See [[Moon Phase Discs]].
- Passing back through the [[Moon Plaza]] next morning, the Moonstone sat in its mount, the fountain was cracked and dry, and the [[Moon Deva]] was not visible.
- Destinations offered by the GM: the Hall of Knights (south), the [[House of Song]], the Market of Many Peoples, the [[Hall of Scrolls]], the Noble Quarter (directly ahead) and the High Mage Quarter (north of it). Pug chose the High Mage Quarter.

## Long Rest at the Garden of Long Memory

- Rested at the southern edge of the [[Garden of Long Memory]]. Pug's suggestion of resting in the Moon Plaza was dropped: the deva had told them to hurry.
- Watches: Herschel, Morwyn, Ripper and Mar. Pug skipped a watch.
- Heard by all on watch: an occasional rattle (transcribed as “canes”; almost certainly chains, as in Session 3) from the garden, followed by a very low laugh.
- Mar, to the southeast: hooves on cobblestones, whips cracking, and humanoids crying out under the lash.
- Ripper, close to the south: warrior oaths or chants of the kind used in nightly rituals. Ripper's player linked this to the Hall of Knights and House of Song.
- Morwyn, directly south: the most beautiful female voice he has ever heard, operatic and wordless.
- Herschel heard nothing beyond the shared sounds. The rest passed without interruption.

## The Road North

- Group Stealth roll: 11.
- Mar scouted (Perception 16). The Hall of Knights is a silent, crumbling ruin. The Noble Quarter is terraced and overgrown, smells strongly of rot, and shows shadowy movement on its high terraces. Nothing impeded the escort.
- The High Mage Quarter is almost completely destroyed: skeletal frames of buildings, scorched and broken ground, embers still smoking.

## Pug's Vision: The Living Quarter

- On crossing into the [[High Mage Quarter]], Pug saw it restored: white towers and bridges, the Mythal at its centre, students and masters of every race at work, and archmages performing a great working that the Mythal answered.
- An old mage teaching children a butterfly cantrip looked straight at him: “Remember this. Magic was never the glory of Myth Drannor. We were its stewards.”
- Final thought left in his mind: _What is preserved must be passed on. What is powerful must be made to serve. And what is lost can still be remembered._ The party is treating these three lines as clues.

## The Heartstone of the Mythal

- Pug's Perception 14 revealed the [[Heartstone of the Mythal]]: a half-buried moonstone roughly 30 feet across with a crystal tree rising from it, shedding glowing moonflowers that never wither. Its trunk is fractured, limbs sheared away and roots splintered or severed. It still pulses like a slow heartbeat.
- Wild magic ceases entirely near it. “You have come to the bedside of a dying guardian.”
- Ocreata (History 15): this is where Myth Drannor's fall began, where the fiends imprisoned above the city came down and the first gates opened. He has avoided it for seven centuries out of shame.
- Herschel (Nature): the trunk is crystal limbs grown from the stone and twisted together in the shape of an old-growth tree, unlike any species he knows.
- GM lore: three fiends were imprisoned above the city in a pocket dimension. Centuries later a prophecy came true, they were released onto the city, and gates opened in the Mage Quarter for their waiting armies.
- Pug needs a fountain in a Mythal city for one of Denlor's spells. The vials remain for the pool of radiance.

## The Shattered Observatory

- Pug (Nature 18, helped by Mar): the central platform is a single slab of moonstone covered in purely observational star charts, recorded “for those who come after”, with no divination.
- Pug copied the charts with calligrapher's tools and his Wizardly Quill (tool check 23).
- Herschel (Investigation 17) found working crystal lenses and the loose [[Polished Black Stone]].
- Experiments with the stone:
  - Torchlight (Ocreata) and a lens (Herschel) had no clear effect.
  - Ripper saw a magnified starscape and the vast darkness between stars, felt the stone warm and caught movement; her next Perception was a natural 1 and left her dizzy.
  - Herschel (Intelligence 5) likened it to a village salesman's “moving pictures”.
  - Morwyn saw stars moving across the surface and called it a micro galaxy.
  - Mar (Insight 18 with Guidance) recognised the skies over Myth Drannor and Cormanthor, but at a different time, moving at the stars' real pace.
- Mar (Investigation 9 with Guidance) identified a meridian pillar. Its Espruar inscription, read by Ocreata: _The heavens need no witness. We watch not for their sake, but for our own._
- When Pug held the stone, matching constellations flared in it and on the platform until only the **Moonboat** remained. A silver line from its brightest star crossed a map of Myth Drannor and ended at the Temple of Sehanine Moonbow; a point beneath the temple filled with light and became a pool. Inscription, read by Ocreata: _The heavens mark the hour, the dead bear witness, and the waters receive the promise._
- Pug (Insight): all his visions come from one decade, the third Coronal's coronation. A Coronal-to-be had to go beneath the temple and spend time there before ascending. Why is unknown.
- Gallery: Ocreata boosted Pug (Athletics 14 with advantage). Pug found the [[Observatory Apprentices Journal|apprentice's journal]]. Legible line: _Master says every answer creates another question. And tonight, I finally understand why that comforts him._ Pug has no Mending. He avoided casting Feather Fall because of the wild magic.

The Heartstone asked: “Its keepers are gone. Are you here as conquerors, or to assume care?” Herschel answered first: “We wish to repair the damage.”

- Outcome: the cracks' spread slowed, hundreds of new moonflowers bloomed and silver light flowed up the trunk. The Mythal is not restored, but has been reminded that people worthy of its trust remain.
- The roots curled around Pug's feet. He felt the life of the city: plaza, knights, House of Song, Hall of Scrolls, the Noble Quarter's forum, gardeners.
- The voice: “Stone falls, songs fade, and kingdoms end.” … “But memory survives.” … “Only if someone chooses to carry it.”
- The city's instability eased and the bleeding was staunched. Pug heard the title **Keeper of Memory**, and accepted it aloud. There was no further reaction.

## The Great Way Circle

- Pug (Arcana 19): a teleportation circle whose energy stops wherever runes are broken or missing.
- Herschel read the runes: seven concentric rings for the principles of travel, **Curiosity, Humility, Wisdom, Fellowship, Responsibility, Trust and Return**. Outer rings damaged, inner rings nearly intact. Activation attempts never repeat.
- Restoration would need more than one mage and much time, with no guarantee of stability. Left unrepaired.
- Herschel and Mar (Perception; Herschel 20) found discs bearing the sigils of Semberholme and Tangled Trees, the other cities of Cormanthyr, both believed ruined.
- Pug took one stone from the circle (agreed retroactively at session end) as a component for Denlor's spells. See [[Great Way Circle Stone]].

## The Collapsed Crystal Conservatory

- Ocreata (History 15): one of Tel'Mythar's most beloved institutions, a living archive of magical plants. Every apprentice tended its gardens.
- Investigation: Pug 19; Ocreata 3; Morwyn rolled a natural 1; another companion rolled a natural 20.
- Features: silver crystal canopy; channels of clear water from the river; a central grove with a chiming silver-leaved tree; a kneeling stone inscribed _To tend one life well is to honour all life_; a sheltered vine of living silver moonflowers.
- Ripper found a stone cabinet of tiny crystal containers; most empty, some with [[Ancient Seeds]]. Herschel (Nature 3) cannot identify or confirm their viability and is carrying them.
- Pug found and took the [[Bronze Purifying Vessel]]. Without it the conservatory's water is ordinary river water. He plans to return it if no better use emerges (the Heartstone and the Pool of Far Seeing were floated).
- Herschel observed that none of the surviving plants compete with one another.

## What the Party Learned

- **Stewardship.** The city's strength was a shared existence in which no part mattered more than the whole. This seems to be what Myth Drannor wants remembered.
- **The fall.** Ocreata understands that the city lost this before it fell: nobles who rejected its philosophy gained influence, elves placed themselves first in the knightly orders and the academy, archmages pursued personal power, and the Knights neglected the populace's safety, so far more died than had to.
- **Hubris.** Pug believes hubris caused the fall and speculates that the third Coronal may have been involved. Unconfirmed.
- **GM hint.** The major test in the High Mage Quarter is complete. The rest is about learning what the city was and what remains.
