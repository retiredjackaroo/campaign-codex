# The Grove of Mercy

_Side Quest, Session 2 Notes_

**Summary:** The escort finishes the were-boar fight it was caught in at the end of Session 1, then travels on to camp at an ancient shrine of [[Eilistraee]] deep in [[Cormanthor]]. There they uncover a [[Harpers|Harper]] drop site hidden beneath a stone bench, complete with the unsettling [[Aurelien Voxs Journal|journal of Aurelien Vox]] and a [[Harper Compass|compass]] that points straight at [[Myth Drannor]], and then witness a moonlit vision of drow refugees being welcomed by surface elves. Full story in [[Session 2 - The Grove of Mercy - Narrative|the narrative]]; verbatim table talk in [[Session 2 - Transcript|the transcript]].

**Previously On...**

Session 1 left the escort mid-combat in a mid-wood glade, ambushed by a hunting party of were-boars while trying to slip past them. [[Ripper]] had charged the shaman solo and gone down prone and badly hurt. Session 2 opens in that same fight. For everything up to the ambush (Pug's audience with [[Elminster]] and [[Sylune]], the solo trek through the Rimwood, meeting the escort), see [[Session 1 - Escort to Myth Drannor - Narrative|Session 1]].

**The Were-Boar Glade (conclusion)**

The fight ran roughly half the session. Key beats:

- [[Herschel Blackroot]]'s Spike Growth and [[Mar]]'s Moonbeam were already down when play resumed. Herschel dashed around his own thorns to Healing Word [[Ripper]], then dropped concentration on Spike Growth as a free action to avoid being shoved into it after a shaman's gore.
- [[Mar]] (the party's centaur) was dropped to 0 by a frenzying boar and revived with a Cure Wounds (20 HP).
- [[Kjeorn]] carried the melee: Morningstar plus Divine Smite, a Shield Bash to knock a boar prone, and a finishing blow.
- [[Morwyn Sabbath]] cast **Crown of Madness** on a green were-boar and directed it to kill the orange were-boar beside it, the first time the spell has ever worked at this table.
- [[Ripper]] cleared her Wrath of the Wild aura (only the shaman failed), uncorked her ever-smoking bottle to blanket the glade in a 60-foot cloud, and used the cover to reposition, whip the red were-boar (Hunter's Mark, speed reduction, disadvantage), heal off it with Hungering Might, and dagger it in the eye.
- The surviving boars fled. The dead reverted to human form.

**Aftermath and Short Rest**

Ripper came out of the smoke shaken and human again, and bummed a cigarette off Morwyn. [[Herschel Blackroot]] used Animate Dead on a reverted were-boar corpse to make a spore-thrall he named **Snotty**; [[Kjeorn]] (Oath of the Ancients) decided this was fine because fungus is nature. Kjeorn also cast Find Steed for a Celestial stag and Prayer of Healing for a short rest's worth of recovery, topping everyone back up.

**The Shrine of [[Eilistraee]]**

The escort reached an old shrine none of them had ever actually entered, inside the perimeter they normally keep others out of.

- A roughly 100-foot circular grove where the forest "chose not to grow", a mirror-still reflecting pool at its centre, weathered columns, worn stone benches, and a marble arch carved with a crescent moon and scenes of welcome (dancers, extended hands, a sword laid down).
- The arch's inscription, in an ancient Cormanthyran dialect, reads: _"No soul who seeks the moon's mercy shall be turned away."_ [[Pug DAstibar|Pug]] read it fluently, [[Kjeorn]] adeptly, Morwyn caught root words, Herschel not at all.
- The shrine is dedicated to [[Eilistraee]], and was historically a refuge site for drow escaping the Underdark.

**The Harper Cache**

Detect Magic flagged an enchantment over the whole grove and stronger auras (enchantment, evocation, divination) under one misaligned stone bench that the grove's roots deliberately avoided. [[Mar]] questioned the plants (Speak with Plants) to no useful end beyond confirming the plants were rude. [[Kjeorn]] forced the bench, which clicked and rotated 180 degrees, dumping him face-first into the moss and revealing a hollow and a chest: a [[Harpers|Harper]] drop site marked with a crescent harp, a leaf-wrapped dagger and a watching eye.

Contents:

- Harper field notes on waxed vellum (undated).
- 10 potions of healing and 1 [[Potion of Heroism]].
- A silver Harper pin (harp wrapped in ivy).
- An inkwell.
- The [[Harper Compass]] (points at [[Myth Drannor]] / its [[Myth Drannor|Mythal]], divination).
- [[Aurelien Voxs Journal|Aurelien Vox's journal]].

Pug identified the compass and kept it as the party's navigation aid, and deliberately left the journal and field notes in place, since a Harper cache is meant to stay stocked. Potions were distributed around the group; Kjeorn took the [[Potion of Heroism]].

**The Vision**

After Pug secured the camp with Mordenkainen's Private Sanctum (kept lit, at Mar's request, so she could still see the stars), the reflecting pool rippled inward and the grove filled with translucent spirits: drow refugees welcomed by surface elves and centaurs, cloaks and bread shared, a choir of both peoples singing together in the old dialect, a drow child dancing with a moon elf. The overwhelming impression was of hope and a "sacred mercy" that persisted at Myth Drannor even during its wars. The vision faded with the pool going still; the party was left too moved to sleep straight away.
