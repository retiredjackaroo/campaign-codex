# Session 5 - Notes

_Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 5_

The combat begun in Session 4 ended in disaster narrowly averted. The escort fought the Binder and its mephits in the [[Garden of Long Memory]], but every member except [[Kjeorn]] fell. Kjeorn freely exchanged his life and soul for the lives and liberty of Pug, Ripper, Mar and Morwyn. See [[Session 5 - The Price of Memory|the narrative]].

## Present

[[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]], [[Kjeorn]], [[Mar]], [[Morwyn Sabbath]], [[Ripper]] and [[Bullshit]]. [[Herschel Blackroot]] was absent from the battle.

## Combat Record

- Ripper marked and whipped the Binder twice, then continued fighting while grappled and prone.
- Kjeorn and Bullshit fought the mephits at close range. Kjeorn killed the magma mephit, smote the Binder, was revived by Ripper and held the final line when everyone else fell.
- Mar dropped Wild Shape but remained chained; she used Barkskin, Guiding Bolt, Archer Form and Starry Wisp while resisting the Binder's control.
- Morwyn's Crown of Madness triggered a catastrophic memory of the garden's fall rather than affecting the Binder; he escaped with Misty Step, then repeatedly kept the group alive with healing, including a fourth-level Mass Healing Word.
- Morwyn destroyed Blue, a dust mephit, with Vicious Mockery: first its hygiene (“you smell like Ripper's socks”), then a whispered performance review, performance-improvement plan and threat of dismissal. Blue screamed itself into ash and dust.
- Pug changed Fireball into thunder damage with his Awakened Spellbook. The thunderball destroyed a fountain, killed a dust mephit and dispersed three captive spirits. Their ultimate fate is unknown. He later killed the last green mephit with Mind Sliver.
- Pug used the [[Silver Brooch]] to summon a red-feathered blood hawk. It missed its attack on the Binder and was destroyed in a burst of feathers.
- Ripper spent Heroic Inspiration to give Morwyn another chance at an important heal, then used a healing potion to revive Kjeorn before the Binder knocked her unconscious.
- The Binder dragged Mar and Ripper, used chained spirits to terrorise the party, and repeatedly offered a deal for one captive companion.
- The group dealt roughly 100 damage in one decisive round after the spirits no longer supported the Binder, but could not finish him.
- Bullshit was destroyed in the fight. Pug, Ripper, Mar and Morwyn were rendered unconscious.

## Kjeorn's Sacrifice

- The Binder offered Kjeorn the lives and free will of the four fallen companions in exchange for one soul.
- Kjeorn refused to surrender Pug or any friend, signed the contract himself, and was killed and made into the Binder's chained shade.
- The paladin's final peace took the form of an oak-leaf light. The GM identified it as a boon from [[Silvanus]]: Kjeorn cannot be saved from bondage by it, but will not feel pain in it.

## End State

- The four surviving escorts will wake alive in the Garden of Long Memory.
- Kjeorn is gone with the Binder. He tried to drop his backpack only after the bargain had already taken effect, so no equipment is confirmed to have remained behind.
- The Binder is wounded but active, with Kjeorn now bound to it.
- No action by Herschel or Snotty was recorded. Herschel's imagined return carrying mushrooms was table banter, not an event in the story.

## Next

- Learn what happened while the survivors were unconscious and honour Kjeorn.
- Consider whether Kjeorn's shade can be recovered from the Binder.
- Continue toward [[Sehanine Moonbow]]'s temple and the pool of radiance.
