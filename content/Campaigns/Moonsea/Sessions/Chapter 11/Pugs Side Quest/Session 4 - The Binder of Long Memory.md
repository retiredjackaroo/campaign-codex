# Session 4 - The Binder of Long Memory

*Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 4*

## Out of the Tunnels

The battle beneath the [[Grand Avenue]] resumed with two [[Ankhegs of Myth Drannor|ankhegs]] still trying to carry the escort underground.

[[Ripper]] stabbed Herschel's captor before racing after [[Morwyn Sabbath]]. [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] climbed from the first tunnel and dropped a Fireball behind the creatures. The blast killed the ankheg holding [[Herschel Blackroot]] and scorched another nearly senseless.

[[Mar]], still a giant eagle, seized Morwyn's captor and wrenched it bodily from its hole. Morwyn remained in its jaws, playing his lute and singing as both rose thirty feet into the air. Mar released them. The ankheg struck the avenue hard enough to lose its grip; Morwyn rolled free, bruised but delighted by the flight.

The last determined ankheg caught [[Kjeorn]] and repeatedly tried to reach its tunnel. Herschel answered each retreat with Thorn Whip, dragging creature and captive back into daylight. Ripper slowed and carved at it, Mar blocked the hole, Morwyn restored the wounded with Mass Healing Word, and [[Bullshit]] healed Kjeorn through a touch of its nose. Kjeorn finally forced the mandibles apart, fell out headfirst and gave the others a shaky thumbs-up. Herschel cracked lightning through the shell, and Ripper slid underneath to drive her sword through the soft underbelly.

The avenue fell quiet. Kjeorn's Prayer of Healing gave the battered escort the benefit of a short rest.

## The Cracked Heart of the City

During the fighting, Pug's Mind Sliver had called up another vision of the living city. Beneath it came something new: a vast rose-white moonstone, cracked across its surface, with a tree of moonstone branching from it in full stone bloom. Its fractures pulsed as his magic moved through the city.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Moon Crystal.png]]

Pug recognised that Myth Drannor's reactions were not behaving like ordinary wild magic. The vision suggested that the city's strange memory echoes and unstable spellcasting were tied to a wounded source beneath it, but revealed neither its name nor its location.

Then the escort heard chains.

## The Garden Remembered

Ripper tracked the faint metallic sound northeast into the [[Garden of Long Memory]]. Mar carried Morwyn above the trees while the others passed through crooked iron gates into a garden both beautiful and profoundly wrong. Ancient paths had become moss, hedges had become walls of wild growth and memorial stones stood everywhere. Some were wrapped in rusted chains; others bore grooves worn by iron dragged over stone.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Garden Memory.png]]

The garden remembered itself for them. Paths gleamed, fountains leapt and families picnicked under perfect hedges while a citywide celebration approached. A child asked whether they would see the Coronal. Apprentices hurried past with white flowers. Ordinary citizens believed tomorrow would be better than yesterday.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/Pugs Memory - Coronal Day.png]]

The silver breeze returned. The people vanished. Only the fountains continued, waiting for families who never came home.

Herschel deciphered part of one memorial: **Eliana Starblue, gardener and friend**. Its broken poem preserved only three reliable words—*flower*, *song* and *crumble*.

Pug proposed removing a chain from another marker. Kjeorn tried to unwind it, then used a crowbar. The rusted iron snapped and fell around the stone with a clatter.

“It's a terrible idea,” Pug had said. “Let's do it.”

## Warm Ones

The birds stopped. The fountains seemed to hesitate. Iron and ash entered the air.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Binder of Long Memory.png]]

[[The Binder of Long Memory]] emerged through the trees: a towering devil crowned in shackles, living black chains coiled around him and five captive spirits—men, women and children—straining from his bonds. Four elemental mephits skittered at his feet.

“Warm ones,” the Binder said. “After so long. How delightful.”

Kjeorn charged first. His morningstar struck, but wild magic swallowed his Shining Smite and teleported him into the reflecting pool. He climbed out furious and attacked a fire mephit with Bullshit beside him.

The Binder sent a bound spirit against Pug. Flowers withered into vines and restrained him. Ripper stepped between them, loosed Wrath of the Wild and frightened two mephits before attacking the devil. Herschel's Confusion later caught one of the elementals but had no effect on the Binder.

Above the canopy, Morwyn and Mar did not immediately realise combat had begun. When they returned, they did so at speed: Mar swept down through the trees while Morwyn stood on her back, lute in hand, singing “Danger Zone.” Mar grappled an earth mephit and carried it skyward.

Morwyn's upcast Dissonant Whispers barely troubled the Binder, but his recovered Cutting Words spoiled one of the devil's chain strikes against Mar. A later chain caught her eagle's leg, dragged her twenty feet down and slammed both eagle and bard into the earth. Mar kept hold of the mephit. Morwyn landed prone beside her.

The session ended there: Pug had broken free of the vines, Kjeorn and Bullshit faced the elementals, Ripper and Herschel held the garden, and the Binder stood among five chained souls. The escort agreed on one thing—the spirits might be prisoners, and they should not be harmed if they could be freed.

## Loose Ends

- Combat is live in the [[Garden of Long Memory]].
- [[The Binder of Long Memory]] controls five chained spirits and four elemental mephits.
- Mar and Morwyn are prone after being dragged from the air; Mar still holds an earth mephit.
- One mephit is affected by Herschel's Confusion.
- The escort wants to free the spirits if possible.
- Myth Drannor's memory echoes may be tied to a cracked moonstone source beneath the city.
- The pool of radiance and the temple of [[Sehanine Moonbow]] remain ahead.
