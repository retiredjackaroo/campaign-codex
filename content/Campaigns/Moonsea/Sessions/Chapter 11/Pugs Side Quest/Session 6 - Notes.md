_Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 6_

Herschel and the newly arrived Ocreata found Pug, Morwyn, Ripper and Mar alive after Kjeorn's bargain. The party honoured Kjeorn, learned that Ocreata has been trapped in Myth Drannor since its fall, then reached the Moon Plaza and began solving its lunar mechanism under attack from a Moonstone and Moon Deva. See [[Session 6 - Beneath the Same Moon|the narrative]] and Morwyn's complete memorial song, [[Over the Hills and Far Away]].

## Present

- Active: [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]], [[Herschel Blackroot]], [[Morwyn Sabbath]] and [[Ocreata]].
- Present in the fiction but unplayed for most of the session: [[Ripper]] and [[Mar]]. They began unconscious, recovered off-screen during the group's excursion, and entered the Moon Plaza at the closing cliffhanger.
- Posthumously central: [[Kjeorn]] and [[Bullshit]].

## Recovery in the Garden

- Herschel returned from gathering shell fungus, moss and flowers to find the others bloodied and unconscious and Ocreata checking their pulses.
- Ocreata tried to stop Herschel casting Healing Word because of Myth Drannor's unstable magic. Herschel evaded him but accepted the warning.
- Herschel spent two healer's-kit uses to stabilise Ripper and Mar, then two healing potions to wake Pug (4 HP) and Morwyn (6 HP).
- A chained memorial stone bearing Kjeorn's name stood over the survivors. Neither Kjeorn nor the Binder was present.
- Morwyn concluded that Kjeorn had accepted the Binder's bargain. Pug vowed to research a way to recover him; Morwyn promised to write a song.
- Morwyn cast Aid successfully after the city's percentile check, choosing Pug, Mar and himself by coin toss. After the long rest he cast it again on Pug, Herschel and himself.

## Ocreata

- A scarred drow in ancient, torn armour, armed with a glaive.
- Came to Myth Drannor as a child refugee and remembers the city before its fall, when its streets held people, songs and laughter.
- Failed to defend the city and ran. Myth Drannor will not let him leave or rest until he repays his debt.
- Has died repeatedly in the ruins and awakens where he fell after an unknown interval. He had not realised roughly seven centuries had passed.
- Believes the city seeks a worthy bearer for its memory and is testing Pug in particular.
- Guarded the party during their long rest and led them through the ruins.
- Ocreata wept when Morwyn sang because he had not heard music in the city for more than seven hundred years.

## Kjeorn's Song

Morwyn performed [[Over the Hills and Far Away]], honouring Kjeorn's journey, sacrifice, bondage to the Binder, Silvanus's protection and Bullshit. He then added the final Herschel verse supplied in the complete song.

## Route Through Myth Drannor

- The party sheltered in a ruined building outside the named districts. Herschel heard chains and distant laughter throughout the eight-hour rest.
- They left Ripper and Mar concealed with a note while the four active characters continued toward [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow|Sehanine Moonbow's temple]].
- They skirted the Garden, passed the [[Hall of Scrolls]] and rejected a detour after learning its guardians still endured.
- On [[Coronals Way|Coronal's Way]], they investigated a smoking, brimstone-filled crater. Morwyn connected it to the legend of three fiends released from a pocket dimension and falling on Myth Drannor like meteors after the third Coronal's crowning.
- The temple's Espruar inscription reads: “May every soul find its path beneath the same moon.”

## Pug's Coronal-Day Memory

- Entering the [[Moon Plaza]] gave Pug a vision of the restored city during the third Coronal's investiture.
- Twenty-four ceremonial knights formed an honour guard. Memories of the first and second Coronals accompanied the newly anointed third.
- The third Coronal knelt before the people and declared that the honour was service, not dominion.
- The city shared responsibility for its future. The Coronal appeared to look directly at Pug while speaking of that trust.

## The Fountain Puzzle and Combat

- A person-sized [[Moonstone]] hovered above the central fountain. The party identified it as a magically conductive component of Myth Drannor's Mythal.
- Nine sockets ring the fountain: one contained a full-moon disc; eight were empty.
- Herschel found a polished new-moon disc, placed it opposite the full moon, and caused the Moonstone to pulse.
- The Moonstone attacked with radiant lightning, blasts of force and area bursts.
- Ocreata warned that the crystal was not the true guardian and used Dodge to shield Morwyn and Herschel.
- Morwyn's Dissonant Whispers showed no visible effect on the crystal.
- A [[Moon Deva]] manifested before Herschel and wagged a finger at him.
- Pug successfully Counterspelled the deva's Bigby's Hand.
- Morwyn used Command (“Grovel”); the deva resisted and answered “No” in the same musical key. Morwyn then gave Herschel Bardic Inspiration.
- Herschel summoned a fey hawk. The unstable magic placed it across the plaza, but it successfully marked two likely disc locations before the deva bound it with liquid moonlight and destroyed it in the fall.
- Pug summoned [[Nick Timoney]] to search. Nick's natural 1 produced an elegant flight with backward rolls but no discoveries.
- Ocreata charged the deva and used Action Surge for four glaive attacks. All were deflected, but the glaive's Graze mastery dealt 4 damage each time and drew blood. The deva then shoved Ocreata twenty feet and knocked him prone.
- The deva ended the session by placing a hemispherical Wall of Force around the fountain. Ripper and Mar entered the plaza at that moment.

## End State

- Combat remains active in the Moon Plaza.
- The fountain and its fitted lunar discs are enclosed by Wall of Force.
- Full moon and new moon are fitted; two further possible discs were located but not recovered.
- Ripper and Mar have rejoined the group.
- The temple doors remain unentered, the underground route remains unknown, and the six Crystal Vials remain empty.

## Next

- Survive or satisfy the Moon Deva and complete the lunar mechanism.
- Enter the temple and find the route beneath Myth Drannor.
- Learn whether Ocreata's aid can repay his debt to the city.
- Find a way to recover Kjeorn from the Binder.
