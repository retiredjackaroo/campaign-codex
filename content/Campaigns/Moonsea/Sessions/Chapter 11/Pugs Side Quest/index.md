## Where We Are Now

The escort reactivated the [[Moonstone]]'s ward and travelled north into the devastated [[High Mage Quarter]]. A star stone at [[The Shattered Observatory]] showed the pool of radiance lying beneath the [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]]. At the dying [[Heartstone of the Mythal]], every companion answered its question, slowing its decline, and it named Pug the Keeper of Memory. The quarter's main test is complete; the escort is now piecing together what Myth Drannor wants remembered.

## Loose Ends

- Pug has accepted the title Keeper of Memory. What it obliges him to do, and whether it binds him to the city, is unknown.
- The GM confirmed the High Mage Quarter's main test is complete. Unexplored there: Tel'Mythar, the Academy of High Magic; the Pool of Far Seeing; the Broken Lecture Gardens; the Ruined Archives of High Magic; the Fallen Archmage's Walk.
- The pool of radiance lies beneath the [[Temple of Sehanine Moonbow]]. A Coronal-to-be had to spend time below the temple before ascending; why is unknown.
- One lunar disc has been removed to keep the Moonstone guarding the temple. The weakened [[Moon Deva]] still bars entry until the city judges the party.
- Pug is collecting components for Denlor's half-completed spells: one teleportation-circle stone of three, possibly the apprentice's journal, and water from a fountain in a Mythal city.
- Pug has the conservatory's [[Bronze Purifying Vessel]] and means to return it if no better use emerges. Herschel carries the unidentified [[Ancient Seeds]].
- Night sounds not yet investigated: chains and a low laugh in the Garden of Long Memory, whips and cries to the southeast, warrior chants and a beautiful singing voice to the south.
- [[Kjeorn]] is bound as [[The Binder of Long Memory]]'s shade. Pug has vowed to research a way to recover him.
- [[Ocreata]] cannot leave or permanently die in Myth Drannor until he repays his debt to the city.
- The six [[Crystal Vials]] remain empty; the pool's water must not touch Pug.
