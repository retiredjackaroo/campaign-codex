# Session 3 - Enter as Guests, Leave as Guardians

_Chapter 11, Side Quest, Session 3_

> [!quote] The Moon Arch
> _Enter as guests. Leave as guardians._

## The Starwood

The next morning, the forest changed scale.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Starwood.png]]

The Midwood gave way to ancient oaks broader than cottages, disappearing into a canopy hundreds of feet overhead. Old roads survived as whispers between enormous ferns. Fallen trees had been cut from the paths but left to feed the soil; streams bent around elder roots; broken markers had been raised and bridges remained maintained. The elves had made a covenant with [[Cormanthor]]. [[Myth Drannor]] had fallen. The forest had kept its half.

[[Kjeorn]] summoned his celestial elk, [[Bullshit]]. [[Morwyn Sabbath]] immediately tried to enchant Bullshit into licking Kjeorn's face for eight hours. Bullshit resisted, leaving Morwyn down a spell slot and everyone else richer for having seen him try.

[[Herschel Blackroot]] kept [[Snotty]] beside him. The animals showed no alarm at the spore-thrall. [[Pug DAstibar|Pug D'Astibar]] led with the [[Harper Compass]] and [[Silver Brooch|Sylune's silver brooch]]. Elk paced him. Birds flew ahead and called as he approached. A snake slipped between his feet before vanishing. Whether they recognised the brooch or something in Pug himself, the animals were heralding him.

Near mid-afternoon [[Nick Timoney]] returned with news of stone structures ahead.

## The Moon Arch

The [[The Moon Arch|Moon Arch]] stood at the edge of Myth Drannor, carved from pale moonstone. Pug touched the cold stone and went weak at the knees. This was no copied illustration. The Tel'Quessir had lived here. The city he had loved from afar had been real.

"We found it," he managed. "It's here. It's real."

When the party tried to pass, moonlight ran through the arch and became a silver barrier. Three plinths rose from the ground.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Moon Arch Test.png]]

[[Mar]] helped Pug take rubbings of their worn Espruar inscriptions. With Comprehend Languages and his wizardly quill, Pug recovered three tests.

The first asked the visitor to speak and let **memory** judge them. Pug offered the dream that had changed his life: [[Phlan]] falling and [[Mantors Library|Mantor's Library]] under attack. Before that night he had chased whatever interested him. After it, he knew he had to save his city.

The second asked the visitor's **purpose**. Kjeorn declared his purpose to protect nature, protect Pug on his quest and preserve Myth Drannor against corruption.

The third said, _Take nothing that you would not first protect. Offer stewardship and be welcomed._ Herschel understood. He entrusted his quarterstaff—walking stick, weapon and the tool through which he had channelled the forest—to the city.

The barrier cleared. Kjeorn hoisted Pug high enough to read the outer inscription:

_Enter as guests. Leave as guardians._

Pug crossed first. On the inner face another message waited:

_Remember us kindly._

## The City That Waited

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The City of Song.png]]

Beyond the arch, birdsong fell away. Broad avenues ran between districts reclaimed by ivy and oak. Marble bridges crossed clear streams. Towers stood wrapped in flowering vines; enormous trees rose through shattered halls; stairs climbed to nowhere and unsupported arches refused to fall.

Herschel's quarterstaff reappeared in his hand as he crossed. The city had accepted its stewardship and returned it.

For Pug, the ruins arrived already named: the [[Hall of Scrolls]], [[Garden of Long Memory]] and [[House of Song]]. Every page he had turned had become stone around him. The scars were just as real—melted marble, fallen towers and immense claw marks—but Myth Drannor did not feel dead. It felt interrupted, waiting and remembering.

The warning in [[Aurelien Voxs Journal|Aurelien Vox's journal]] returned with new force. The party chose the temple of [[Sehanine Moonbow]] as their eventual destination, because Vox's expedition had found stairs beneath it and Pug's pool of radiance lay underground. For now they followed the [[Grand Avenue]].

## The First Memory

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The First Memory.png]]

For one heartbeat, the broken avenue was whole. White moonstone shone like still water. Banners carried the silver laurel of Cormanthyr and the crescent moon. Thousands filled the boulevard: elves, humans, dwarves, halflings, centaurs and drow moving openly as honoured guests. Merchants arranged stalls. Children carried moonflower wreaths. Musicians prepared on balconies.

The city was preparing for celebration. There was no fear. Only hope.

Then a rumble passed beneath the street. Music and people disappeared. The avenue returned to broken stone and moss.

[[Mar]] heard chains clinking somewhere northeast. Before she could fix the direction, she noticed the fountain ahead: its basin lifted from its foundations, paving stones torn up as if something had emerged below.

## What Dwelt Below

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/The Fountain.png]]

The avenue exploded. Four enormous [[Ankhegs of Myth Drannor|ankhegs]] burst from collapsing tunnels, mandibles snapping and acid reducing marble to sludge.

![[Campaigns/Moonsea/Assets/What Dwells Below.png]]

[[Ripper]] transformed first and moved between them, striking with her whip and trying to draw them away. Pug attempted to turn one into a cat. It resisted Polymorph, and Myth Drannor answered instead: for Pug alone, the whole city came alive—the avenue, market and Garden of Long Memory—then vanished.

Morwyn cast Slow across all four creatures, catching two. Mar attempted Barkskin; instead, birdsong followed her and flowers burst from the paving. Vines swallowed a ruined column and crushed it. She took giant-eagle form and attacked from above.

Kjeorn marked one ankheg with Shining Smite. Bullshit added a less impressive but wholly sincere blow. Herschel awakened his spores, strengthened his returned staff with Shillelagh and joined Snotty in the melee.

One ankheg seized Kjeorn and began dragging him into its tunnel. Another caught Herschel. Ripper followed Kjeorn, enchanted her whip and tore open the creature's shell.

Pug jumped down after them. He raised his spellbook and sent a Chromatic Orb through the exposed carapace. Lightning tore the ankheg apart. It released Kjeorn and spilled green ichor over him.

"I'm quite enjoying this hole," Pug decided, and stayed there.

Kjeorn climbed out and tried to pull Herschel free. His hands slipped. Bullshit tried next, with the same result. Ripper, Kjeorn and Herschel badly wounded the creature holding him, but it remained alive.

Then another ankheg seized Morwyn and began pulling him toward a second tunnel. Mar struck as it moved but could not stop it. The session closed with Morwyn at the lip of the hole, Herschel still grappled, one ankheg dead and the fight unfinished.

"Holy shit," Morwyn observed.
