# The Templars

Agents of the sorcerer-kings of Athas, the Templars serve as the administrative and enforcement arm of the city-state's ruler. On Athas, Templars draw their magic directly from their sorcerer-king, making them both bureaucrats and dangerous spellcasters. They are a constant threat to anyone operating outside the law.

During Act II, Session 4 a contingent of Templars formed part of the field camp occupying the plaza above [[The Alliance]]'s hidden network, with patrols moving in and out of the camp. Their discovering the cistern entrance would be catastrophic for the resistance below.

**Status:** Active; hostile; ruling Raam (Act II, Session 11)\
**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 4/Session 4 - Narrative|Session 4 - Narrative]]

**Session 11:** [[Leviath]] reports that the Templars' grip on [[Raam]] is now absolute. The Ivory Palace is their bastion, and [[High Kuatogagh Jahandar Shah|Jahandar Shah]] rules openly with the clerics of [[Dregoth Cultists|Dregoth]] as the state religion. They display [[Korno]] daily above a gate to the Templar district, guarded by at least half a dozen Templars and several priests of Dregoth. [[The Voices Amulet|The Voice's Amulet]] proved to bear the insignia of the Templar order that oversees Raam's tanneries and dyeing industries.
