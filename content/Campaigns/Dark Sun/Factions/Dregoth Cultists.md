# Dregoth Cultists

Followers of Dregoth, the undead sorcerer-king of Giustenal, a city-state destroyed long ago and now a ruin beneath the earth. Dregoth himself is a dracolich of immense power who survived his assassination and now plots his return and revenge against the remaining sorcerer-kings. His cultists pursue his agenda on the surface world.

Their presence in the plaza field camp alongside [[The Templars]] and [[Noble House Soldiers]] makes them one of three hostile factions the party must navigate in Act II, Session 4.

**Status:** Active; hostile; Raam's state religion (Act II, Session 11)\
**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 4/Session 4 - Narrative|Session 4 - Narrative]]

## Session 11

![[Session 11 - Sermon of the Crucible Truth.png]]

The clerics of Dregoth, who call themselves disciples of Dregoth, now lead [[Raam]]'s state religion. They wear heavy black robes and ceramic masks painted bronze, walk openly among the people, and are treated as untouchable and revered. People credit them with miracles, including resurrections, cleansed wells and the healing of blight, and their following grows daily. They preach Dregoth as Raam's saviour, demanding sacrifice and absolute fealty. A holy day, the first festival dedicated to Dregoth, has been declared, and [[Korno]] is to be sacrificed on it.

Their sermon, the Sermon of the Crucible Truth, teaches that suffering is the law of flesh and that those who serve without question will rise again after death, "unbroken, unburned, and unyielding": "We do not flee pain, we honor it. We do not resist order, we endure it. We do not beg the elements, we become them. So kneel in dust, bleed for now, and rise forever." See [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 11/Session 11 - Notes|Session 11 - Notes]].

## Session 12

The DM clarified that there was never a person named Dregoth in Raam. The cult takes its name from what it follows: a severe, conservative strain of the old sorcerer-king creed, which calls Dregoth "the Reborn" and "the Returned". Crude shrines of stone and clay, idols warped into dragon-like shapes, now stand openly in Raam's open spaces. Robed acolytes preach in the avenues, promising salvation to the faithful and retribution to the disobedient, and converts swell the Templar patrols. The cult preaches to the slum labour gangs morning and night. Its message is that the city must be rebuilt in readiness for the "Voice of Dregoth" to speak, as the sign that Dregoth has accepted Raam. The city will then be restored on a holy day, Restoration Day, sanctified by the sacrifice of [[Korno]]. No one the party has met knows what the Voice of Dregoth is.
