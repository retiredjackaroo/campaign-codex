## Mother Calls

The chorus grew louder as the party pushed into [[The Colony]].

![[The Colony - Roll20.png]]
_The wet, resin-worked passages of the colony._

[[Derfel]] and [[Kit-Chac]] took the warm southern descent first. Humidity, musk and damp resin opened onto a spiralling pit whose walls were crowded with countless translucent egg sacs. About a dozen adult [[Neogi]] clung protectively around the brood. Their many voices pleaded that they were few, that they wanted only to survive, and that the intruders should come no closer. Derfel tried anyway. Even with the newly attuned [[Ring of Psionic Resistance]], a psychic command stopped him in his tracks. Shaken by the force of it, he and Kit-Chac withdrew and warned the others away from the nursery.

The northern route crossed above the main passage and led into a chamber where chalk-white dust seemed to breathe. Chitinous ribs shimmered with each psionic pulse, web bridges crossed a lightless chasm, and a cocooned male Templar hung on the far wall. His wasted face was exposed; a ceramic chain vanished beneath the silk. The party somehow knew that [[The Voice]] was either the source or a conduit for the chamber's crushing chorus.

![[The Voice.png]]
_The Voice, suspended within the hive's webbing._

The pressure struck hard. [[Anselem]] and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] reached three levels of hive strain and became deafened beneath the thoughts in their heads. [[Salt]] reached two levels and lost the speed to react; [[Babs]] gained one. [[Pterrence]], Kit-Chac and Derfel resisted.

Anselem wanted the noise stopped. He hurled his first Fireball before the cocooned man could speak, while Derfel's two arrows arrived in the same instant. The silk burned away and the Templar fell screaming in a voice that was neither human nor beast. Pterrence caught the body with Levitate; Tchk'Tchk battered the hanging ruin with telekinetic force. Still burning, the Voice warned that free will was a facade, that the hive offered true continuation, and that refusal would be punished.

The warning became a mass compulsion. Tchk'Tchk and Salt snapped themselves free with psionic discipline, but Anselem and Kit-Chac turned at once for Mother's chamber. The others wrestled them back repeatedly: Derfel caught Anselem by the collar, Babs grappled Kit-Chac, and Pterrence joined the dogpile while Salt contributed what he called an aggressive hug. Even [[Brave]] charged Kit-Chac on Derfel's command, only to bounce from the Thri-Kreen's carapace.

Before leaving, Salt crossed the sagging web bridge and used Mage Hand to strip a ceramic amulet from the levitating corpse. Its insignia marked the Voice as a Templar responsible for a garment or tannery district of [[Raam]]. When Pterrence finally released Levitate, the charred body vanished into the chasm.

Then Mother's servants arrived: two Neogi and a [[Hook Horror]]. Kit-Chac and Anselem saw allies sent to collect them; everyone else saw a fresh attack. Pterrence shredded one Neogi with Cloud of Daggers while Derfel marked it and drove two arrows home. Babs resisted a mind hook, released Kit-Chac long enough to charge the enemy, then seized Anselem whenever he tried to leave. Salt's telekinetic attacks struggled to find their mark, but he helped contain Anselem and later reached across the fight with an extended psionic limb.

Tchk'Tchk ended the first Neogi in a single movement: he lifted it bodily and threw it into the bottomless chasm. He then Commanded the hook horror to halt while Pterrence's daggers, Derfel's arrows and Babs's blade tore into it. When the command expired, Tchk'Tchk hurled the monster into the same darkness. The remaining Neogi could not stop Kit-Chac. With Step of the Wind he raced out of sight, following Mother's call.

The party let Anselem pull against Derfel's grip and used his compulsion as a guide. Kit-Chac reached [[The Hive Mother]] first. Her chamber curved like the belly of a beast, dripping with glistening threads. Broodlings crawled over her bloated body; two adult Neogi attended her, and another hook horror waited nearby. Mother offered stillness, certainty and belonging. Kit-Chac answered, “My clutch is where I thrive,” resisted her attempt to dominate him, and charged.

![[The Hive Mother.png]]
_The Hive Mother at the heart of the colony._

Alone for two rounds, Kit-Chac struggled to pierce Mother's defences. Her attendants pinned him with psychic hooks and punished every act of resistance, while Mother filled him with terror. He held his ground until the rest of the party arrived.

![[Session 9 - Mothers Chamber.png]]
_The party reached Mother's chamber with Kit-Chac already surrounded._

Derfel marked Mother and struck her with an arrow, then dodged both hooks of the creature charging him. Babs used Second Wind and moved to hold the hook horror in place. Pterrence battered it with Magic Missile. Mother lashed Babs's mind, flooded the chamber with psychic force and compelled nearly everyone to throw away the weapons in their hands.

Anselem obeyed by casting his sword aside—then placed a Fireball over Mother, one Neogi and the hook horror. The explosion wounded all three, though Mother held her concentration. Salt finally landed the telekinetic strike he had been chasing all night, breaking the restraint on Kit-Chac, then flung the hook horror away from the front line. The Neogi rushed back in, but Kit-Chac's Patient Defence turned aside every bite.

Tchk'Tchk crossed the chamber and called, “Swap out.” Kit-Chac chose not to resist. One psionic wrench later, he stood beside his clutch again, clear of Mother and her attendants. Tchk'Tchk hardened himself with biofeedback and withdrew with him.

The Hive Mother still lives. Her chamber is full of eggs, webbed cocoons, two adult Neogi and a wounded hook horror. The fight remains unfinished, but the party has recovered Kit-Chac and opened a chance to retreat.
