## Summary

The party explored deeper into [[The Colony]]. [[Derfel]] and [[Kit-Chac]] found a vast southern nursery: a spiralling pit lined with countless egg sacs and guarded by about twelve adult [[Neogi]]. A psychic command stopped Derfel despite advantage from his attuned [[Ring of Psionic Resistance]], and the scouts withdrew.

![[The Colony - Roll20.png]]
_The colony's resin-lined interior._

The northern route led into a psionically resonant chasm chamber. [[Anselem]] and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] reached hive strain 3, [[Salt]] reached 2, and [[Babs]] reached 1. Anselem and Derfel immediately burned and shot a cocooned Templar called [[The Voice]]. [[Pterrence]] caught the falling body with Levitate, allowing the Voice to deliver a final warning and a mass suggestion before Salt recovered [[The Voices Amulet|The Voice's Amulet]] with Mage Hand.

![[The Voice.png]]
_The cocooned Templar known as the Voice._

The suggestion compelled Anselem and Kit-Chac to go to Mother's chamber. The party repeatedly grappled them while fighting two Neogi and a [[Hook Horror]]. Tchk'Tchk telekinetically threw both Neogi and the hook horror into the chasm. Kit-Chac escaped restraint and reached [[The Hive Mother]] two rounds before everyone else.

![[The Hive Mother.png]]
_Mother waited in the deepest chamber reached this session._

Mother failed to dominate Kit-Chac, but her attendants restrained him and she frightened him. When the party arrived, Derfel wounded Mother, Babs engaged the hook horror, Pterrence struck it with Magic Missile, and Anselem caught Mother, one Neogi and the hook horror in a Fireball. Salt broke Kit-Chac's restraint with a telekinetic strike and pushed the hook horror away. Tchk'Tchk then used Telekinetic Propel to pull Kit-Chac back to the party.

![[Session 9 - Mothers Chamber.png]]
_The battle in Mother's chamber at the end of Session 9._

The session ended mid-combat. Mother, two attendant Neogi and a wounded hook horror remain active. The party has recovered Kit-Chac and may be able to retreat.

## Party contributions

- [[Derfel]] — scouted the nursery and survived its psychic command; used the Ring of Psionic Resistance throughout; helped destroy the Voice; restrained Anselem; wounded Mother with Hunter's Mark and his longbow; directed Brave against the compelled Kit-Chac.
- [[Babs]] — resisted a Neogi mind hook; repeatedly grappled Kit-Chac and Anselem to contain the mass suggestion; fought the first hook horror and engaged the second after reaching Mother's chamber; used Second Wind twice across the two combats.
- [[Pterrence]] — used Levitate to preserve the falling Voice long enough for the party to recover its amulet; controlled the first fight with Cloud of Daggers; joined the effort to contain Anselem; damaged the second hook horror with Magic Missile.
- [[Kit-Chac]] — scouted both branches with Derfel; was compelled to reach Mother's chamber; resisted Mother's attempted domination; survived two isolated rounds against Mother and her attendants; held the line with Patient Defence until Tchk'Tchk extracted him.
- [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] — resisted the Voice's mass suggestion; threw both Neogi and a hook horror into the chasm; used Command to deny the hook horror a turn; pulled Kit-Chac out of Mother's reach with Telekinetic Propel and fortified himself with biofeedback.
- [[Anselem]] — confirmed his new level-5 power by opening with Fireball against the Voice; endured hive strain 3 and a mass suggestion; guided the party toward Mother while restrained; placed a second Fireball across Mother and two of her defenders.
- [[Salt]] — resisted the Voice's mass suggestion; helped contain Anselem; recovered the Voice's amulet with Mage Hand; broke the Neogi restraint on Kit-Chac and pushed the second hook horror away from the party.
