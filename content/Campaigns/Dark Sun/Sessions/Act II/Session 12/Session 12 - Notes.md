The party finally met [[Master Avariah]], who asked them to put the [[Gulbadan Estate]] ahead of [[Korno]] and search its ruins for the secret of Raam's water. They refused to abandon Korno, and agreed instead to do the estate first and the rescue after. [[Salt]] stayed below at Avariah's request. Back on the surface, the party found [[Neith]] in the slums and, through her, Korno's last captain, [[Jalen Marek]], whose fighters will stage a diversion. For the full story, read [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 12/Session 12 - Narrative|Session 12 - Narrative]].

This is the final session of Act II. Act III begins next session.

**XP awarded:** the DM awarded 500 XP base to everyone for finishing the act. The persuasion skill challenge with Avariah failed, so it earned nothing. Individual awards from rolls were 60 XP for [[Kit-Chac]], 40 XP for [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]], 60 XP for [[Babs]], 40 XP for [[Pterrence]], 30 XP for [[Anselem]] and 30 XP for [[Derfel]] (530 total). Salt received the 500 base only. [[Pterrence]] reached level 6.

## Summary

[[Leviath]] led the party most of a day deeper beneath [[Raam]], away from the old Neogi lair, to a cavern lit by pale green fungal mats around a glowing pool. Avariah was waiting there.

![[Session 12 - The Masters Pool.webp]]

- **Salt singled out.** Avariah asked to be introduced to Salt personally, called him "one of [this world's] rarest beings", and said she did not believe his arrival was an accident.
- **The tablets and the water.** Avariah confirmed she knows of the vaults beneath the Ivory Palace, and showed a stone tablet salvaged from them. It speaks of Rajaat, a great mortal of the age when Athas was a paradise, who mastered pure, preserving magic. She believes the water beneath Raam does not dwindle as House Gulbadan claimed and must be fed by a tremendous source.
- **The tapestry.** [[Babs]] recalled the Gulbadan tapestry mapping Raam's aqueducts: every line led to a water source except those to the Gulbadan estate, which ran off the map. [[The Padshah]] had dismissed this as artistic licence. Avariah thinks the Padshah may have lied.
- **Her request.** Find the Gulbadan secret instead of risking themselves for Korno, "whose loyalty to the aims is unproven". If an everlasting source exists, she intends to release it publicly, so that no one, the Alliance included, controls it.
- **The appeal.** Pterrence, Salt, Derfel (backed by Babs) and Kit-Chac each argued for Korno; Tchk'Tchk floated teleporting him out; Anselem declined to press. The skill challenge failed and Avariah did not change her position.
- **Terms agreed.** After Derfel told her bluntly that the party trusts people, not institutions, and saw no conflict in helping both, Avariah set terms: do the Gulbadan estate (and the vault, if attempted) before any rescue, free Korno from wherever he is held at night rather than from the wall by day, then go underground to the pool on the route to the Hidden Spring and wait for her to make contact through Leviath. Because the party's knowledge would endanger it, she will relocate the [[The Hidden Spring|Hidden Spring]] cell.
- **Salt stays.** In a private conversation (not on the recording), Avariah asked Salt to remain and help with work on the springs and water. He told the party she is genuine in her belief, though his read on her was poor, and that she is far older than she looks. He accepted. A quiet elf, a cleric of the air from the Hidden Spring, went up with the party in his place (see Open threads).
- **Supplies.** The cell gave each party member two potions of healing or one greater potion of healing, plus non-magical gear from its stores.
- **The surface.** The party climbed out of a dead well in the arena markets near [[The Furnace]] at night. Raam is repaired but subdued: work gangs by day, dragon-like shrines to Dregoth in the open spaces, acolytes preaching in the avenues, and Templar patrols reinforced by converts. Leviath could go no further without being recognised.
- **Rumours.** Kit-Chac overheard labourers saying the Templars keep a heavy presence in the slums because food is still hidden there, guarded by Korno's loyalists. Others said Korno looks healthy and restored each morning: "They want him strong for the sacrifice."
- **Neith.** Found half-starved in a tent city of forced labourers in [[The Slums]]. She told them of a room behind a false wall near where the Gulbadan manor joined the collapsed entablature, which the Padshah visited seldom and always alone. She refused to leave because her fellow labourers would be punished for her absence, but led the party to Jalen.
- **Jalen Marek.** [[Grainseed|Ammat Grainseed]] guided them to a hidden safe house where Jalen and roughly half a dozen fighters remain. He will stage a diversion outside the Templar walls. If Korno cannot be freed, he wants him killed rather than sacrificed. Anselem set the timing: Jalen's people will be ready from midnight tomorrow and can be in position within an hour of word arriving through Neith and Ammat.

## What the party learned

### Master Avariah

- Tall, in desert silks; appears human but older than she looks, possibly with some elven blood (her ears are large but not quite elven). Her eyes have a celestial light with something sharper beneath.
- Carries a sceptre of bone spiralled with obsidian, topped by a dark crystal whose inner flicker pulses like a heartbeat.
- Recited the Alliance's five aims as: to preserve life, to oppose defilers, to protect knowledge, to safeguard the hidden, and to train the next generation. (This differs from the list the party was given by the Hidden Spring cell in Act II, Session 5; see Open threads.)
- Describes the Templars as defilers. Eats no meat.
- An Alliance cell (not the Hidden Spring) previously reached the Ivory Palace vault by tunnelling from underground, after a long search. She would not use that route again and expects the vault to be watched or trapped.

### The Gulbadan secret

- The estate is in the noble district, not the Templar district.
- Per Neith: beyond the grand hall where the party was raised into the Padshah's household lay the family residence. Near where the manor joined the entablature (the raised servants' wing, now collapsed) is a room hidden by a false wall, leading to an area the Padshah visited seldom and always alone.
- Servants' rumours said it housed a djinn who granted the Padshah wishes, or a place of dark family rituals. Neith rejects the darker rumours: she believes it was solemn and sacred to the family.

### Korno and the cult

- Korno is hung on the wall above a Templar district gate by day and held inside the district at night. Witnesses, including Jalen, say he appears healthy and restored each morning.
- He is to be sacrificed on a holy day the party now knows as Restoration Day. Its date depends on the "Voice of Dregoth" speaking to the city as a sign that Dregoth has accepted it; the slums are made to rebuild in preparation. Nobody the party has met knows what the Voice of Dregoth is. The projected voice of the three masked clerics who delivered the Sermon of the Crucible Truth may be connected.
- Jalen Marek, one of Korno's lieutenants, now leads the remnant of the slums resistance.

## The plan

1. Get fresh work papers from [[Chazhal|the Pitch]] or the Nightrunners in [[The Elven Market]].
2. Search the [[Gulbadan Estate]] for the hidden room.
3. While in the Templar district, try the vaults beneath the Ivory Palace for remaining tablets.
4. Free Korno from wherever he is held at night, under cover of Jalen's diversion. Kit-Chac's reasoning: once Korno is out, getting back in will be far harder, so do everything in one push.
5. Retreat underground to the pool on the route to the Hidden Spring and wait for Avariah's contact through Leviath.

## Party contributions

- [[Derfel]]: stood at the back of the meeting, untrusting. Argued that a living Korno keeps the Templars distracted, that Avariah would have to defend the water forever against strong Templars, and finally told her the party trusts people, not institutions, and can help both her and Korno. Called [[Grave]], whom he left with Salt during the private meeting. Revoked Pterrence's decision-making privileges at Jalen's safe house.
- [[Salt]]: singled out by Avariah on arrival. Argued that Korno matters because the people and the Templars both treat him as important, and that a public sacrifice serves nobody's aims (persuasion failed). Met Avariah alone and agreed to stay with the Alliance. Gave most of his ceramic to Pterrence for a spell focus before parting.
- [[Pterrence]]: made the party's opening case to Avariah, raised the Ivory Palace vault, and asked whether the secret could restore life to Athas. Produced the Pitch's papers and asked for help, securing potions, gear and the lead on Neith. Ritual-cast Detect Magic to check Salt after his private meeting (nothing beyond his own items). Took a stealth-friendly chitin breastplate in place of his scale mail. Blurted the plan aloud in Neith's hovel and had his beak grabbed for it. Reached level 6 and plans to learn Clairvoyance and Fireball.
- [[Kit-Chac]]: "Water for all is a dream for the soft-shelled. Korno is pack. Pack is now." Blended in with a natural 20 on the walk into the slums and overheard the rumour about hidden food and Korno's loyalists. Explained the party's purpose to Jalen, and argued for doing the estate, the vault and Korno in one push.
- [[Babs]]: recalled the Gulbadan aqueduct tapestry. Sized Avariah up and backed Derfel's argument. Took a simple glaive from the cell's stores.
- [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]]: noted the "binary choice", suggested splitting up or teleporting Korno out, and judged that Templar psionicists would likely anticipate it. Used Alter Self to look as average as possible on the surface. Asked Jalen about Korno's daily restoration.
- [[Anselem]]: declined to argue with Avariah. On the surface, limped along with Pterrence's disguise. Advised Pterrence to keep his trap shut, led the talk with Jalen, pledged to see the rescue through, and fixed the timing: tomorrow night, midnight.
