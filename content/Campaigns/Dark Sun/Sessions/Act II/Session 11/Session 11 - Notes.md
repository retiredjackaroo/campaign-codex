The party escaped the collapsing Neogi colony with three of its members unconscious, returned to [[The Hidden Spring]] as heroes, and learned that [[Korno]] is alive and will be sacrificed at Raam's first festival to Dregoth. They agreed to try to free him. The session ended with [[Master Avariah]] summoning the party to meet her. For the full story, read [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 11/Session 11 - Narrative|Session 11 - Narrative]].

**XP awarded:** 500 XP base for the escape skill challenge, plus individual awards: 630 XP for [[Kit-Chac]], 590 XP for [[Salt]] (his rolls were made by Pterrence's player), 570 XP for [[Derfel]], and 560 XP each for [[Pterrence]] and [[Anselem]]. [[Babs]] and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] were unconscious throughout the challenge and received no award.

## Summary

The session opened with a retcon to set up the escape. The whole party was placed at the mouth of [[The Hive Mother]]'s chamber: [[Anselem]] had come back, [[Kit-Chac]] had rejoined, and [[Salt]] had freed himself from the rubble. [[Babs]], [[Pterrence]] and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk]] were unconscious. The DM ruled that hive strain no longer mattered with Mother dead, but everyone who had carried it now had one level of exhaustion (−2 to rolls); [[Derfel]] alone was clear. Pterrence and Salt's existing exhaustion was folded into the same −2.

![[Session 11 - The Hive Collapses.png]]
_The colony comes apart after Mother's death._

With Mother's psionic link severed, [[The Colony]]'s resin structures crumbled and its Neogi and enthralled creatures collapsed as their minds shattered. The party ran a skill challenge to reach the vent, each success moving them 15 feet: Derfel carried Pterrence, Anselem carried Babs, and Kit-Chac carried Tchk'Tchk.

- **Round 1:** four successes; the party covered the full 60 feet. In a group Perception check, Derfel and Kit-Chac heard a faint, persistent hiss somewhere behind them, like gas escaping under pressure.
- **Round 2:** two successes, two failures. Anselem ran into a collapsing wall (4 bludgeoning) and Kit-Chac was thrown prone when the floor split. The party entered the wide corridor leading to the vent, and a boulder sealed off the way back to Mother's chamber.
- **Round 3:** three successes. Derfel ran into a new rock wall (5 bludgeoning), and Pterrence's staff was jolted loose and lost; nobody stopped for it. A chasm opened short of the vent. A group Perception check picked up a great roiling roar and a crash from deep in the colony, like a massive wave of force driven through a tight space.
- **The leap:** all four cleared the chasm, and the party climbed through the vent into the old slave pen, which was outside the colony's heart and stable.

![[Session 11 - Anselem Carries Babs.png]]
_Anselem carries Babs through the collapsing tunnels._

With no healer's kit and no spells left, Anselem improvised a poultice from minerals and lichen melted with his own heat, and revived all three unconscious party members at 2 hit points. Derfel called his new companion, [[Grave]]. The party rejoined [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]], [[Koresh Ironblood|Koresh]] and the freed prisoners, who were starting to show awareness of their surroundings. A ritual Detect Magic sweep of the slave pens found only the bone dagger and spellbook already recovered. Over the next couple of days the party travelled back to [[The Hidden Spring]] and was fully restored.

At the Spring the cell now treated the party almost as members. [[Sarath]] offered them an open invitation to join and confirmed they were free to leave. [[Leviath]] brought news from [[Raam]]: Templar rule was absolute, a state religion of Dregoth was spreading, and [[Korno]] was alive, displayed daily over a gate to the Templar district and marked for sacrifice at the first festival of Dregoth. The party agreed to rescue him. Sarath asked for time to consult [[Master Avariah]], and [[Nisat of the Cavern Moss|Nisat]] sent a dragonfly messenger. The next evening Avariah's voice answered through the grotto pool, summoning the party through Leviath. Play stopped before the meeting.

## The state of Raam

As reported by [[Leviath]]:

- **[[The Templars]]** hold the city absolutely. The Ivory Palace is their bastion, and [[High Kuatogagh Jahandar Shah|Jahandar Shah]] rules openly.
- **The Nightrunners** still operate in [[The Elven Market]], but their leaders have slipped away, some say beyond Raam's walls.
- **House M'Ke** is now a licensed merchant house, governing trade and caravans in return for oaths of fealty to Jahandar.
- **The nobles** have been reduced to privileged subjects sworn to Templar law.
- **[[The Slums]]:** survivors have been enslaved or conscripted into labour camps, joined by others who refused to bow. Patchwork resistance continues in places.
- **[[Dregoth Cultists|The clerics of Dregoth]]**, in black robes and bronze-painted ceramic masks, now lead the city's state religion. They are untouchable and revered, credited with resurrections, cleansed wells and the healing of blight, and their following grows daily.

![[Session 11 - Sermon of the Crucible Truth.png]]
_A masked cleric of Dregoth._

The DM re-read a sermon the party had previously heard from one of these clerics. It teaches that suffering is the law of flesh, and that those who serve without question will rise again after death, "unbroken, unburned, and unyielding," into a kingdom of eternal will.

## The plan for Korno

- Korno is chained daily to the wall above the main gate to the Templar district, on a busy thoroughfare between the Elven Market and the trade districts, and taken back inside each evening.
- The gates are open by day and guarded by at least half a dozen Templars and a few priests of Dregoth, with more close enough to respond.
- Contacting Korno psionically or magically is judged too risky: the Templars would likely detect, block or misdirect it.
- The festival's date is unknown. Leviath believes there is time, since the city is still rebuilding after the Inundation.
- The forged work orders from [[Chazhal|the Pitch]] may be out of date; the Nightrunners still have a presence in the Elven Market if he needs to be found again.
- Working plan: a small distraction to clear civilians, then a larger explosive (possibly a cart) to draw the Templars, Pterrence's Fog Cloud, and a snatch of Korno in the confusion. Leviath insists on keeping civilians clear.
- Tarek will quietly procure explosives through the cell's craftsmen and Nisat's knowledge of volatile materials, without telling Nisat why. If the mission goes ahead, the cell will abandon the safe house and leave signs for the party to follow.

## Party contributions

- [[Derfel]]: carried Pterrence out of the colony; succeeded on Athletics and Survival through the first two rounds, then ran into a rock wall in the third, losing Pterrence's staff; cleared the chasm with a natural 20; called his new companion [[Grave]]; argued against the Korno rescue as suicide before agreeing to it; commissioned a +1 longbow from Pterrence and told him not to mess it up.
- [[Salt]] (rolled by Pterrence's player): freed himself from the rubble; cleared falling debris telekinetically, read the cave structure to choose safe passages, extended his limbs with psionic energy to outpace the others, and crossed the chasm on telekinetically placed stepping stones, spending both Luck Points and his heroic inspiration; made the group persuasion pitch to Tarek.
- [[Anselem]]: carried Babs out; led a clear path by Survival (natural 20), ran into a collapsing wall in the second round but kept hold of her, then forced his way through and crossed the chasm; revived all three unconscious party members with an improvised poultice; argued for the Korno rescue; took and attuned [[The Voices Amulet|the Voice's Amulet]].
- [[Kit-Chac]]: carried Tchk'Tchk out by turns in his arms, his mandibles and on his back; succeeded in the first round, was thrown prone when the floor split in the second, then succeeded in the third round and on the chasm leap; heard the hissing sound with Derfel; framed the rescue as the clutch's decision and Korno as part of the pack; drew a map of the gate where Korno is displayed; offered Pterrence money to learn spells.
- [[Pterrence]]: unconscious for the escape; woke with a lump on his head and no staff; ran a ritual Detect Magic sweep of the slave pens; argued for rescuing Korno and suggested Fog Cloud cover for the snatch; used Portent; carved a new obsidian-tipped staff and began a +1 longbow for Derfel; handed the skull fragment to the cell for safekeeping.
- [[Babs]]: unconscious and carried out by Anselem; revived by his poultice. Her player was absent.
- [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]]: unconscious and carried out by Kit-Chac; revived by Anselem's poultice. His player was absent. The bone dagger he has been using was identified.

## Party status at session end

All party members are conscious and fully restored after the journey back to [[The Hidden Spring]]. Hive strain has been replaced by one level of exhaustion for those who carried it; whether the long rests on the return journey cleared it was not stated. [[Grave]] has replaced [[Brave]] as Derfel's companion. Pterrence has a new staff, but his original was lost in the colony.
