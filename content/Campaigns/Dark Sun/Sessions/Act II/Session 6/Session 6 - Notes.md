# Session 6: Notes

**XP awarded:** ~540 base per character, plus small individual bonuses (variously reported between roughly 60 and 175 depending on character, tied to skill-challenge success/failure and features used); treat the bonus figures as approximate, as the GM's own running totals shifted more than once while reading them out.

## Summary

The session opened with the party settling into life at [[The Hidden Spring]] under the Alliance's request that they remain in the sanctuary for now. [[Salt]] learned that refugees who saw him in the water during the Session 5 cloaker fight have spread word of a water genasi in their midst; no one has confronted him directly, but he can feel the sanctuary's awareness of it. [[Pterrence]] offered to craft [[Derfel]] a magic longbow given time and coin (crafting rules TBC), and traded his 2nd-level spell Locate Object to [[Sarath]] for Mirror Image. A group charisma check about the party's patience over [[Korno]] was won, memorably, by [[Brave]].

Word came that two Alliance scouts, Narek and Scylla, had missed check-ins after investigating a psionic disturbance in unexplored northern reaches of the aquifer. [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek "Cricket" Silvertwig]] volunteered the party to find them, both as a genuine search and an unstated test of trust; [[Koresh Ironblood]] came along as well. [[Derfel]], [[Babs|Her]], [[Pterrence]], [[Anselem]] and [[Salt]] agreed and set out with Tarek and Koresh into the northern tunnels. **[[Kit-Chac]] and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] were absent this session.**

A skills challenge crossing the tunnels found a scrap of Scylla's torn clothing but otherwise went badly: [[Pterrence]] disturbed a patch of mushrooms and was poisoned, both he and [[Salt]] misjudged flooded pools and picked up levels of exhaustion, and only [[Babs|Her]]'s find of the fabric scrap counted as a genuine success. After an uneventful night's camp, the party reached a chamber lined with biological, mucus-slicked resin and heard a faint, ever-present telepathic hum. An [[Umber Hulk]] carrying two [[Neogi]] burst from the ground and attacked.

The fight ran long and dangerous. The Umber Hulk's confusing gaze dazed [[Anselem]] more than once, and the Neogi's psionic hive-mind briefly seized both [[Babs|Her]] (turned against [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]]) and [[Koresh Ironblood]] (turned against [[Derfel]], landing a natural 20 shield bash to the face). [[Pterrence]]'s Fog Cloud blinded the battlefield for several rounds to deny the Umber Hulk's gaze, at the cost of blinding most of the party too; [[Salt]] kept the party standing with Aura of Vitality and Cure Wounds while blind and unable to fight effectively. Koresh, still charmed the entire fight, ultimately delivered the killing blow to the Umber Hulk. [[Anselem]]'s Guiding Bolt detonated one Neogi outright, and [[Babs|Her]], shaking off the charm, closed the fight by beheading the second.

Searching the aftermath, the party found cocoon-like silk bundles and, after a short rest, [[Derfel]] scouted ahead to a lower chamber and spotted what looked like "a hell of a lot of spiders." The session ended there, with the party about to investigate further.

One other thing came out of this session for good: the moment Babs beheaded the second Neogi, Tarek called her a "bad-ass bitch," and Derfel and Pterrence's players made it official on the spot. The nickname had been floated once before, in Session 5, but from this session on it's her name. See [[Babs]] and the build note below.

## Combat: the Umber Hulk and the Neogi

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Neogi.png]]
_The Neogi: arachnid-bodied, halfling-sized psionic slavers, riding the Umber Hulk they directed telepathically._

An [[Umber Hulk]] burst from the ground carrying two [[Neogi]] on its shoulders (arachnid-bodied, halfling-sized, with long humanoid-faced necks), directing it telepathically. A low, ever-present psionic hum was felt by everyone in the area, human and non-human alike, before the ambush even began.

### What worked

- **Fog Cloud** ([[Pterrence]]) obscured the whole battlefield, denying the Umber Hulk's confusing gaze to everyone inside it, at the cost of blinding most of the party as well; the party debated dropping it mid-fight and eventually did once the gaze threat had been managed enough.
- **Aura of Vitality** ([[Salt]]) provided repeatable 2d6 healing each round to whoever was in range, keeping the party topped up through a long, damaging fight even while Salt himself was blind and largely unable to act offensively.
- **Guiding Bolt** ([[Anselem]]), landed after several failed attempts against the confusing gaze, detonated one Neogi outright, splattering the surrounding party in remains.
- **Beheading strike** ([[Babs|Her]]), immediately after shaking off the Neogi's charm, ended the second Neogi in one blow.
- **The killing blow on the Umber Hulk** came from [[Koresh Ironblood]], who drove his axe through its gut, despite having been charmed against the party for the entire encounter.

### What did not go to plan

- **The Umber Hulk's confusing gaze** dazed [[Anselem]] on at least two separate turns, costing him actions at critical points.
- **The Neogi hive-mind's charm** turned both [[Babs|Her]] and [[Koresh Ironblood]] against their own side mid-fight: Her landed a critical hit and a follow-up strike on [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] before recovering; Koresh, charmed the whole encounter, hit [[Derfel]] with an axe swing and then a natural 20 shield bash that flattened his nose.
- **Fog Cloud's downside**: with the whole area obscured, most of the party (barring [[Babs|Her]]'s blindsight and [[Pterrence]]'s use of Squeaky) could not see to attack effectively either, leading to several wasted or blind-guess actions, including [[Derfel]]'s arrow disappearing uselessly into the mist.
- **The skills challenge earlier in the day** was mostly a failure: one success (Her's tracking) against four failures (a mushroom-spore poisoning, two exhaustion levels from misjudged water, and a stumbled wall collapse), yielding little bonus XP.

### Outcome

Both Neogi and the Umber Hulk were killed. The party, battered, poisoned and variously exhausted, took a short rest afterward rather than press on immediately.

## Rolls and dramatic moments

- **The most charismatic member of the party.** A group charisma check about patience with the Alliance's "stay put" request was topped by [[Brave]], Derfel's dog, to the table's delight.
- **Salt's silent tension.** Salt is aware the sanctuary's refugees now know what he is, and spends the early part of the session visibly resisting the urge to submerge himself in the spring rather than risk further exposure.
- **Koresh's unwitting kill.** Charmed against the party for the entire fight, Koresh nonetheless landed the blow that ended the Umber Hulk, a detail only clocked by the table afterward ("this is when we find out he's still been cursed this whole time").

- **"You are a bad-ass bitch."** Tarek shouted this the moment Babs beheaded the second Neogi, and Derfel and Pterrence made it official on the spot. The nickname had been floated once before, in Session 5's cold open, but this time it stuck for good: she is Babs from here on. See the build note below for how this is reflected across the vault.
