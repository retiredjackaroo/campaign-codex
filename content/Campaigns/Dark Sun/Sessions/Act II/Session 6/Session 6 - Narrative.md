# Session 6: Narrative

Three days into their stay at [[The Hidden Spring]], the party had settled into an uneasy rhythm of rest, patience, and careful cooperation. [[Derfel]] and [[Pterrence]] were content simply to have the space, and [[Salt]] was glad of the water, though not without a cost: word had spread among the sheltering refugees of what they'd glimpsed during the cloaker fight the session before, and Salt could feel the sanctuary's quiet awareness of him even though no one had said a word. He spent his days resisting the urge to submerge himself entirely and waiting, half-expecting one of his own party to finally say something.

No one did, not yet. Instead, [[Pterrence]] offered to cast Invisibility on Salt whenever he wanted to slip away for a proper swim, an offer met with a long, mournful look at the water rather than an answer. [[Derfel]], meanwhile, floated the idea of a new longbow, and Pterrence, citing his Arcana proficiency and woodcarver's tools, offered to enchant Derfel's own bow given time and coin, a plan the two left unresolved for now. Pterrence also used the quiet days to trade a spell from his own book, Locate Object, to [[Sarath]] in exchange for Mirror Image, and swapped out a cantrip while he was at it.

When Sarath gathered the party to gauge their patience with the Alliance's request that they stay put, the group answered warmly enough, if a little anxiously; [[Pterrence]] admitted they'd likely keep pressing Leviath daily for news of [[Korno]] regardless. A charisma check meant to gauge the sincerity of that patience was, to general hilarity, won outright by [[Brave]], Derfel's dog, over every member of the actual party.

Word soon came that mattered more than patience. Two Alliance scouts, a human named Narek and an elf named Scylla, had gone missing exploring unfamiliar reaches of the aquifer north of the Spring's territory, their last message describing a psionic disturbance. Overheard through the cavern's stone, [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek "Cricket" Silvertwig]] volunteered the party for the search before [[Sarath]] had even finished explaining the situation, framing it plainly: they wanted to stretch their legs, they wanted the Alliance's trust, and here was a chance to prove they deserved both. [[Koresh Ironblood]] added himself to the party as well, making no secret that he intended to see what they were really capable of.

[[Derfel]], [[Babs|Her]], [[Pterrence]], [[Anselem]] and [[Salt]] agreed. [[Kit-Chac]] and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] were nowhere in evidence, off on business of their own. The six of them, with Tarek taking point and Koresh bringing up the rear, set out into tunnels the Hidden Spring cell did not control and barely knew.

The aquifer's damp, mineral hush deepened the further they went. Moss and pale underdark algae shimmered faintly on the walls; blind cave beetles crunched underfoot; violet fungal gardens breathed spores into air already thick with the smell of stagnant water. Tarek, delighted despite himself, scraped a sample of the wall growth into a flask as a gift for [[Nisat of the Cavern Moss]], muttering that Nisat would love the disgusting stuff.

![[Northern Tunnels.png]]
_The stretch of aquifer tunnels north of the Hidden Spring, crossed during the day's scouting._

The tracking itself went poorly. [[Babs|Her]] found the only real success of the day, a scrap of torn fabric snagged on a jagged rock that Tarek confirmed as Scylla's. Everyone else fared worse: a handhold gave way beneath Her elsewhere in the tunnels, bringing down a shower of loose stone; [[Pterrence]], searching a mushroom-choked chamber for signs of foraging, disturbed a cloud of spores and came away poisoned for his trouble; and both Pterrence and [[Salt]] misjudged the depth of a flooded stretch of tunnel, each picking up a level of exhaustion in the process, with Salt in particular baffled that anyone would consider merely neck-deep water "too deep." The party camped for an uneventful night, keeping watch in shifts, [[Brave]] included, before Tarek led them on the next morning.

Midway through the second day, the ground began to change. The floor narrowed, the stone giving way to a black, brittle resin ribbed with mucus, scored by long claw-marks. Both [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] and [[Koresh Ironblood]], hardened as they were, drew their weapons and looked unsettled; neither had seen anything like it. Beneath the strangeness of the place ran something stranger still: a faint, ever-present hum, felt rather than heard, settling into every mind present regardless of race or resistance.

[[Anselem]] confirmed by touch and smell that the resin was biological. As the thought was still forming, the ground began to shake.

An enormous shape tore free of the earth: an [[Umber Hulk]], two smaller creatures riding its shoulders, arachnid-bodied and halfling-sized, their long necks ending in disturbingly humanoid faces. Neither made a sound, but their limbs tapped and stroked along the Umber Hulk's neck and shoulders as though issuing silent commands, and the ambient hum sharpened into something almost like a voice.

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Neogi.png]]
_The Neogi: arachnid-bodied, halfling-sized psionic slavers, riding the Umber Hulk they directed telepathically._

The fight that followed ran long and cost the party dearly. The Umber Hulk's gaze, wide and confusing, dazed [[Anselem]] more than once, costing him whole turns as he stood dumbfounded mid-cast. [[Pterrence]] answered by dropping a Fog Cloud across the entire battlefield, blinding the Umber Hulk's gaze along with most of the party, a trade the group argued over mid-fight and only partially made peace with. Through the blindness, [[Salt]] kept the party standing, sustaining Aura of Vitality round after round and landing an upcast Cure Wounds on Her when she needed it most, functionally fighting the battle from inside a healer's fog of war.

It was in that same fog that the Neogi's true weapon showed itself. Their hive-mind reached into more than one head at once. [[Babs|Her]] was told, in a chorus of a thousand voices, that the creature across from her was her master and that her actual allies were the threat; she turned on [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]], landing a critical hit and a follow-up strike before the compulsion broke and she came back to herself horrified at what she'd done. [[Koresh Ironblood]] fared worse and longer: seized by the same hive-mind, he turned on [[Derfel]] mid-fight, first with a wild axe swing and then with a shield bash that connected on a natural twenty, breaking Derfel's nose outright. He would remain charmed for the rest of the encounter without anyone realising it in the moment.

Slowly, the tide turned. [[Anselem]], once he found a moment clear of the Umber Hulk's gaze, loosed a Guiding Bolt into one of the Neogi that struck true and detonated the creature outright, spattering everyone nearby with the remains. [[Derfel]] and [[Brave]] fought in true coordination for the first time, the ranger's Hunter's Mark guiding the dog's own bite in turn. Blind, poisoned, and exhausted in places, the party kept swinging until, astonishingly, it was [[Koresh Ironblood]], still very much charmed against them all, who drove his axe clean through the Umber Hulk's gut and ended the fight's central threat without ever knowing whose side he was actually helping. Moments later, [[Babs|Her]], the compulsion finally broken, closed out the last of the Neogi with a single beheading stroke.

Tarek let out a shout, half awe and half disbelief, as the second Neogi's head hit the ground: "You are a bad-ass bitch." Derfel and Pterrence picked it up on the spot, and between the two of them made it stick for good, it was not the first time the name had come up, Session 5 had floated it once already in passing, but this time it was official. From this point on, she is Babs.

The chamber beyond the fight told its own story. Crumpled silk cocoons lay scattered among the resin, alongside what looked like the remains of a den rather than a lair merely passed through. Tarek confirmed one further identifying detail for Scylla, a ceramic nose piercing, in case Pterrence found the chance to Locate Object for her later. Battered, poisoned, and short at least two levels of exhaustion between them, the party pulled back to a safer stretch of tunnel for a short rest rather than press on blind and bleeding.

Before they settled, [[Derfel]], with Salt's Enhance Ability lending him an edge, crept to a ledge overlooking a chamber below and looked down into a depression split by a raised stone spine, its sides riddled with alcoves. Mummified silk bundles hung from many of them, some slumped forward like sleepers, others simply heaped.

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/The Colony.png]]
_The chamber Derfel glimpsed from the ledge._

He reported back over Salt's telepathic bond, understated to the point of dry humour: it was, he said, a bit spidey. A hell of a lot of spiders, in fact. The party broke for its short rest with that image freshly in mind, the question of Narek and Scylla's fate, and what waited in the chamber below, both still unanswered. </content>
