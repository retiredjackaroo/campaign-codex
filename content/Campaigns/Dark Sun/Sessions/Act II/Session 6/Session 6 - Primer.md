# Session 6: Primer

Prep notes for the upcoming session, picking up from [[Session 5 - Notes|Session 5]] at [[The Hidden Spring]]. This is a planning document, not a played-session record, so it is unpublished by default; fold anything that actually happens into the proper Session 6 Notes/Narrative afterward and flip publish on for those.

## Sanctuary situation

The party has met [[The Alliance|the Veiled Alliance]] in force and been granted sanctuary at [[The Hidden Spring]]. Despite wanting to return to the surface, potentially with Alliance support, they've been asked to be patient: the Alliance won't let people come and go freely until the party is better known and trusted. The matter of [[Korno]] will be raised with the Alliance's overall leader, a figure named **Master Avariah**, likely the same higher, rarely-seen figure [[Sarath]] answers to and who the cell already rumours to be close to Avangion transformation (see [[The Alliance]]). In the meantime, the party is invited to rest and replenish, with patience framed as the best way to earn trust.

Treatment at the sanctuary is civil but distant. No hostility, but most residents keep their distance, aside from [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek "Cricket" Silvertwig]], the halfling ranger already established as the one officer who places no barrier between himself and the party.

## Leviath's surface reports

[[Leviath]] comes and goes over the following days with updates from above:

- [[Korno]] was taken to the Templar district and hasn't been seen since.
- The new regime now rules [[Raam]], though parts of the slums still resist via a more clandestine network than before.
- [[Dregoth Cultists|The Cult of Dregoth]] is growing, with thousands attending morning sermons, though Leviath suspects most attend to avoid Templar trouble rather than out of faith.

## Salt's aquifer exposure

When [[Salt]] fell in the aquifer during the Session 5 cloaker fight, the refugees present also saw the physical signs of his true nature, distinct from the party's own reveal (Kit-Chac, Her and Tchk'Tchk seeing it directly; see [[Salt]]). No one at the sanctuary has approached or said anything to him directly, but Salt notices looks and other subtle signs that something about him is now known within the sanctuary.

Open threads to play out:

- How much the refugees actually understood versus guesswork from looks alone, and how much of that has been passed on to the wider sanctuary.
- Whether anyone, Tarek included, treats Salt exactly as before, or whether even the friendliest face shows a flicker of difference.
- Whether this pushes Salt towards leaving faster, or towards trying to prove something to the sanctuary instead.

## Threads to track going forward

- [[Derfel]]'s patience versus his urgency to reach the surface, in tension with his noble house backstory.
- Whether [[Korno]] gets pursued directly or left to the Alliance's own channels via Master Avariah.
- Raam's slum resistance network as a possible future contact point.
- [[Dregoth Cultists|The Cult of Dregoth]]'s fear-driven compliance as a possible weakness or complication later.
- [[Salt]]'s arc around involuntary exposure, and how it colours the "earn our trust" dynamic for the whole party.
- Carried over from Session 5: the two missing Alliance scouts and [[Koresh Ironblood]]'s offer to bring the party along to search for them.
