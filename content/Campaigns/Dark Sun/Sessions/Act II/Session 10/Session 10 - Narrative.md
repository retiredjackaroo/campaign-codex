## Mother Falls

The battle in Mother's chamber resumed with the party already worn thin. [[Anselem]] and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] still carried three levels of hive strain, deafened and slowed by the chorus in their heads. [[Salt]] carried two, [[Babs]] and [[Pterrence]] one each, and Pterrence and Salt were still dragging the exhaustion they had brought into the colony. [[Derfel]], newly confident in his second arrow each turn, was clear.

![[Session 9 - Mothers Chamber.png]]
_Mother's chamber, where the fight resumed._

Before Derfel could loose, the egg clusters at the back of the chamber pulsed. Two burst open, and thousands of tiny Neogi broodlings poured out across the floor in living carpets.

Derfel kept his Hunter's Mark on [[The Hive Mother]] and put two arrows into her elongated body. Mother wailed aloud, not psionically, filling the chamber with a raw cry of pain. In every mind the hive answered: stop, your attacks are futile, the only alternative is to become nothing, while the brood lasts forever. The [[Hook Horror]] lunged at Tchk'Tchk with both hooks and caught them together, wasting its turn prising itself free. Babs feinted her way past its guard and opened a seventeen-point wound in its thick hide.

Mother answered with Fear. Derfel failed even with his [[Ring of Psionic Resistance]], and Babs and Pterrence failed beside him; only Tchk'Tchk shrugged it off. Derfel's bow, Babs's longsword and Pterrence's staff hit the floor, and all three fled the chamber. The hook horror, caught in the same cone, fled with them.

## Holding the Chamber

Anselem tucked himself behind a luminescent fungal growth for half cover and sent Scorching Ray at Mother. One ray struck her squarely, but her concentration held. When Mother lashed Tchk'Tchk's mind with a single, furious voice, "I gave you a chance", the psychic blow was swallowed by the biofeedback he had grown the session before. Salt stretched out a psionic limb and spent his last Cure Wounds on Babs before she ran.

Tchk'Tchk teleported the frightened hook horror behind a stand of pillars, then dropped Shatter over Mother and her eggs. Eggs popped and the walls cracked, but still the Fear held. A crescendo of white static filled every mind; only Anselem failed to resist it.

Out in the corridor, the fear broke one by one. Derfel steadied himself forty feet from the chamber mouth and whistled [[Brave]] to fetch his bow, and the dog obeyed. Babs came running after him, covered in biting broodlings that tried and failed to burrow into her flesh. Pterrence, slowed by exhaustion, raised Shield against the swarm as he ran and kept running.

Inside, the remaining defenders held. A Neogi taskmaster lunged at Tchk'Tchk and his psionic backlash turned the blow aside and bloodied its mind; a bone dagger's ward turned two more bites later in the fight. Mother tried to Command Salt to grovel, and he refused. Anselem's second volley of Scorching Ray burned her twice more. Then the colony itself shook. Resin ribbing shivered in the rock and a fall of stone half-buried Anselem and Salt, who each tried to pull the other clear and only pulled each other into the debris.

## The Return

Derfel came back first, brushing the swarm off Babs. When the hook horror's fear finally broke its line of sight, it bolted past them down the tunnel; Derfel's shortsword and Brave's teeth sent it limping on. Babs used Second Wind, threw off a second swarm, and walked back into the chamber toward her sword.

Mother's psychic pulse caught Anselem again, stunning him; even her taskmaster froze in the blast. Her voice carried pure fury now: the party had been given a promise and a warning, and had forsaken both. Salt, pinned and out of spell slots, shoved a broodling swarm away with telekinetic force. Tchk'Tchk, his psionic reserves finally spent, gathered Pterrence's dropped staff in one of his spare hands and withdrew down the corridor. Two more egg sacs split open.

Derfel strode back in and found his mark again. One arrow and a hail of thorns tore into Mother, leaving her slick with blood and scorch marks. She let the Fear go, and the hook horror turned on Pterrence in the tunnel. He threw a javelin, took its hooks, and kept retreating until Tchk'Tchk teleported him back toward the cave mouth and returned his staff.

Babs recovered her sword and attacked. Two of four strikes landed, the second opening a long cut where Mother's neck met her thorax. Mother answered with claws and a bite; Anselem's Warding Flare spoiled one blow, but a claw still mangled her. Then the attendant Neogi came to Mother's aid and its venomous bite knocked Babs to the ground. After a long pause she took a knee and slowly stood back up, refusing to stay down, while the Neogi crept closer with its appendages already reaching for her. Anselem's player lost his connection during this exchange, and the table kept the Urokite fighting from under his rocks; his last Scorching Ray missed.

## Mother's Bargain

Babs spat at the Neogi beside her and swung again, and this time the blade took off one of Mother's legs. Mother ordered her attendant to strike, and Babs fell. The Neogi struck again as she lay there, driving her two failures from death.

Mother nudged Babs's body and spoke into every mind. Was this one's life precious to them? Pterrence answered: "Don't kill her, you beast." The dead were no use to the hive, Mother said. She offered a trade: walk out, take the captives already freed, and Babs would not be cocooned but would join the hive and become one of them.

The party argued. Pterrence wanted to take the deal and come back for a rescue. Salt objected that Babs had already spent her life imprisoned by others, then countered: the captives back in exchange for Babs. Mother refused; this was not a negotiation, and because Babs was strong, the hive would restore her and make her one of its own. Derfel tried threat instead: "If you kill her right now, you are dead. All your children are dead, and we are going to go through everything else with fire and burn it down."

"You are the ones that came into our home and started killing things," Mother replied. When Derfel accused her of kidnapping their friend, she asked, "Kidnapping, or enhancing?" Then she cast mass suggestion: stop your attack and leave our lair. Everyone but Salt and Anselem resisted. Anselem struggled free of the rubble and began walking away from the fight. Salt broke the compulsion at the start of his turn with psionic discipline and kept attacking, and Mother's Neogi lifted Babs onto its back.

Mother sighed. They had all chosen to keep their free will, she said, and used it to sacrifice both themselves and Babs. If anyone attacked again, her thrall would kill Babs.

## The Last Arrow

Salt tried to wrench Babs away telekinetically and failed against the Neogi's grip. Tchk'Tchk did not fail: he teleported Babs straight out of its hold. Derfel reached her under the cover of Zephyr Strike, but his attempt to stabilise her failed. A swarm burrowed into Salt's flesh, Pterrence's javelin flew wide of Mother, and Babs held on through her own death save. Salt, realising his Spare the Dying reached thirty feet, stabilised her from under his rocks. Derfel put Hunter's Mark back on Mother, knowing Brave, buried in broodlings, would not last another round.

Mother turned her domination on Tchk'Tchk, and this time he broke. Her command was simple: join the brood, help keep the one they had tried to take, and fight those preventing it. Tchk'Tchk teleported Babs back to Mother's side, cried out that he had her, and flung a needle at Derfel. Mother praised her new thrall and told him to defend the hive.

The tunnels shook again. Rock and rubble crashed down across the chamber. Derfel alone dodged clear. The fall knocked Tchk'Tchk and Pterrence unconscious, buried Salt deeper, crushed one of Mother's attendant Neogi and impaled the hook horror on a falling stalactite. It killed Brave.

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Pasted image 20260627123731.png]]
_Brave, Derfel's third companion, killed by the rockfall in Mother's chamber._

Derfel looked down at the third dog he had lost on Athas, then up at Babs lying beside the creature that wanted to keep her, and stopped thinking about anything else. His first arrow went wide. His second went through the Hive Mother's eye and out the back of her skull, and a hail of thorns came down after it and pinned her to the floor.

![[The Hive Mother.png]]
_The Hive Mother, killed by Derfel's arrow._

Mother collapsed, writhing, and the broodlings scattered. In every mind the hive began to wail. The chamber shuddered; dust and debris rained from the ceiling, and the resin structures around the walls began to crack. Anselem was gone, still walking under Mother's suggestion. Babs, Tchk'Tchk and Pterrence lay unconscious, Salt was pinned beneath the rocks with broodlings still burrowing into him, and Derfel was the last one standing. He went to Salt first and began hauling him out of the rubble as the lair started to come down around them.
