# Session 8: Narrative

## The Mother's Children

The battle in the holding pen resumed beneath the chorus of the hive.

[[Kit-Chac]] arrived last, untouched by hive strain and eager to make up for lost time. He ignored the [[Umber Hulk]]'s gaze, charged the orange [[Neogi]] that controlled [[Anselem]], and cut, bit, poisoned and toppled it in a single furious assault. The hive answered by enthralling him. Anselem fought free of his own master, feeling the hive's strange, soothing presence tear away like a drug suddenly denied, but Kit-Chac drove his githka twice into [[Koresh Ironblood]] before the command would loosen.

The remaining Neogi swarmed [[Babs]], leaving her barely standing, while the Umber Hulk mauled Koresh. [[Salt]] healed the mole despite the danger. [[Pterrence]] drove a javelin through one Neogi, and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] pulled Babs bodily out of the swarm with a psionic warp. The sudden vacuum became a thunderclap: his Shatter tore the orange Neogi apart and left two more reeling. Babs healed herself, rushed straight back in, and took the blue creature's head off before opening the red one with her second stroke.

Anselem finished the last Neogi in a burst of holy fire, reducing it to a glowing statue of ash. Its death ripped the enthrallment from Kit-Chac—but the freed Thri-Kreen's first blind swing at the Umber Hulk struck Koresh anyway. The wounded monster tried to burrow out. Koresh hacked it as it fled, then [[Derfel]] threaded an impossible shot into the narrow hole. An arrow pierced its skull and a Hail of Thorns shredded it underground. The holding pen fell silent.

![[Session 8 - Negroni Neogi.png]]
_Neogi, Negroni: bitter, red, and best served thoroughly crushed._

Eight prisoners waited behind resin ribs they could have stepped through at any time. Among them were [[Narek]] and [[Scylla]], alive but deeply bound to the hive. They showed no relief. Narek asked whether they were being transferred; the others repeated that Mother was waiting. Salt claimed that Mother had ordered them moved and led all eight away from the chamber, with Koresh and [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] guarding their friends. They walked in a steady single file for hours, never hurrying, occasionally murmuring, _They are waiting for us._ Distance did not break their obedience, but the rescue itself was complete.

![[Session 8 - The Prisoners.webp]]
_The rescued prisoners remained physically free but mentally bound to Mother._

The party searched the chamber before leaving. Derfel harvested an Umber Hulk claw and serrated mandible. Tchk'Tchk found fifty ceramic pieces, a polished bone dagger traced with psionic runes, and a wizard's spellbook containing Blur and Tongues among its spells. He passed the spellbook to Pterrence and later attuned to the dagger, learning that it could psionically parry attacks.

Babs discovered a narrow vent high in the wall: barely three and a half feet across and roughly thirty feet long. Kit-Chac crawled inside and found its floor, walls and ceiling carpeted by thousands of pale Neogi hatchlings. They flowed over every seam of his carapace until panic froze him in place. Anselem crawled after him; Babs hauled both of them free, dragging them out beneath a living coat of tiny legs. The party watched the opening throughout its short rest, debating oil and fire, before the hive recoiled in shared horror when Anselem burned the vent with Radiance of the Dawn.

![[Session 8 - The Vent.webp]]
_The narrow passage above the holding pen led deeper into the colony._

After a short rest, the party returned to the scorched passage. Beyond the ash lay a deeper resin-lined network. The southern branch descended into warm, damp musk and more chittering; the north was cooler and crossed beneath an elevated passage. The hive pressed harder as they entered. Anselem reached three levels of hive strain, while Tchk'Tchk and Pterrence reached two and Salt gained his first. Only Kit-Chac remained untouched.

Tchk'Tchk remembered the discipline used in his psionic training: focus on one point until the invading chorus becomes background noise. He produced ninety-nine needles left over from an obsolete telekinetic technique and offered each companion a point on which to fix their mind. Pterrence instead stared through the Veiled Lens of Raam and reduced his strain. Derfel fixed on [[Brave]] and cleared his completely. Babs used pain and the discipline of her blade to clear hers. Anselem heated his carved flame amulet and reduced his strain to two. Tchk'Tchk, despite teaching the method, failed over his own needle; Salt's memory of ocean and home could not silence Mother either.

The rescued prisoners were safe behind them. Ahead, the warm southern descent led deeper into the colony—and perhaps to Mother herself.
