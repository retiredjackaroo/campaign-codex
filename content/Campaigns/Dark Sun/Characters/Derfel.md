# Derfel

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Pasted image 20260627123854.png]]
An Elf Beast Master Ranger. Outlander background. Carries a Longbow and twin Shortswords +1. Defined by his bond with his companion dogs: [[Dave]], [[Maeve]] and [[Brave]], all lost on Athas, and now [[Grave]]. Quietly protective of Brave in a way that is deliberate rather than sentimental: he has learned the cost.

Found in the wastes by [[Kit-Chac]], who taught him to hunt. The bond between them is foundational: everything Derfel knows about surviving Athas traces back to his Thri-Kreen brother. Derfel does not think of Kit-Chac as Thri-Kreen. He thinks of him as his brother. The distinction matters enormously to Derfel and would confuse him if pointed out.

- [[Kit-Chac]]: Thri-Kreen who Derfel has somehow exempted from being Thri-Kreen in his own mind

## Character notes

Derfel is deeply racist by the standards of Athas, which is saying something. As an Elf he carries the full weight of Elven tribalism: suspicion of outsiders, loyalty to those within his circle, and a worldview shaped by a species that has survived the death of a world by trusting almost no one. The contradiction at the heart of his character is that affinity overrides prejudice every time, without him noticing. Kit-Chac is his brother, not a Thri-Kreen. Her is his ally, not a Mul. The racism is entirely sincere and entirely undermined by the man he actually is.
