# Pterrence

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Pasted image 20260627194021.png]]

A Pterran party member. Pterrans are native to the Hinterlands of Athas: a reptilian species rarely seen in the city-states, which makes Pterrence a notable presence wherever the party travels.

**Status:** Alive  
**First appears:** <!-- link to Episode 1 once built -->

## Relationships

- [[Salt]]: fellow caster; the two coordinated illusions and battlefield control during the Act II, Session 4 crossing and elemental fight
- [[Derfel]]: a faint, background friction. Pterrence's wordiness wears on the terse, practical Derfel; Pterrence seems largely unbothered by it. Low-key for now, a thread that may surface later. By Act II, Session 11 it had become what Pterrence calls an uneasy alliance: Derfel carried him out of the collapsing colony, and Pterrence is carving him a +1 longbow.

## Notable moments

- **Act II, Session 4:** The party's battlefield engine. Cast Disguise Self to pose as a Mansivar Templar guard during the slums crossing, and Web to block a corridor against pursuit. In the sand elemental fight his Web proved useless (the creature was largely immune), so he pivoted to Cloud of Daggers, upcast to 3rd level, for sixteen slashing damage a round, then Ray of Frost to slow the elemental so it could not walk clear of the blades. The Cloud of Daggers did the bulk of the damage that killed it. Also used Portent to help land [[Salt]]'s opening Mind Whip, and mended his own cracked Obsidian Warhammer with Mending. Credited [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] with winning the fight.
- **Act II, Session 6:** Traded his 2nd-level spell Locate Object to [[Sarath]] for Mirror Image. Disturbed a cluster of mushrooms during the tunnel skills challenge and was poisoned for it, then misjudged a flooded pool and picked up a level of exhaustion. Dropped a Fog Cloud over the entire fight with an [[Umber Hulk]] and two [[Neogi]] to deny its confusing gaze, at the cost of blinding most of the party too, relying on his familiar [[Squeaky]]'s blindsight to keep acting through it.
- **Act II, Session 7:** Identified the [[Ring of Psionic Resistance]] and used Locate Object to follow ceramic nose-jewellery signals toward the missing scouts. Salt cured his poison, but no removal of his exhaustion was recorded. Ended with one level of hive strain.
- **Act II, Session 9:** Caught the burning body of [[The Voice]] with Levitate, preserving it long enough for [[Salt]] to recover its amulet. Used Cloud of Daggers to control the first Neogi fight and Magic Missile against the [[Hook Horror]] guarding [[The Hive Mother]]. His prior exhaustion and one level of hive strain remained unresolved.
- **Act II, Session 10:** Tried Mind Sliver on [[The Hive Mother]] and then stepped forward to form a line. Mother's Fear sent him running down the tunnel, where he raised Shield against the broodlings and later fought the fleeing [[Hook Horror]] alone with javelin and True Strike until [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk]] teleported him clear. Argued for accepting Mother's bargain and returning to rescue [[Babs]]. Was knocked unconscious by the final rockfall. His exhaustion remained unresolved.
- **Act II, Session 11:** Unconscious for the escape, carried out in [[Derfel]]'s arms, and revived by [[Anselem]]'s poultice with a lump on his head; his staff was lost in the colony. Ran a ritual Detect Magic sweep of the slave pens. Pushed hard for rescuing [[Korno]] and proposed Fog Cloud cover for the snatch. In downtime he found a replacement weapon, carved a new obsidian-tipped staff, began carving a +1 longbow for Derfel, and handed [[The Skull|the skull fragment]] to the Hidden Spring cell for containment. Also played [[Salt]]'s rolls this session.
- **Act II, Session 12:** Made the party's opening case to [[Master Avariah]], raised the Ivory Palace vault, and asked whether the Gulbadan secret might restore life to Athas. Secured healing potions, gear and the lead on [[Neith]]. Ritual-cast Detect Magic to check [[Salt]] after his private audience, finding nothing untoward. Swapped his scale mail for a chitin breastplate, received most of Salt's ceramic towards a Clairvoyance focus, and limped through Raam in disguise. Announced the party's plans aloud in Neith's hovel, emptying it, and had his beak grabbed for it. Reached level 6. The +1 longbow for [[Derfel]] is half done.
