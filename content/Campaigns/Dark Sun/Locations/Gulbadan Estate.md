# Gulbadan Estate

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Pasted image 20260627220322.png]]
The estate and seat of House Gulbadan, the faction that ruled [[Raam]] under [[The Padshah]], with [[The Gulbadan Maulani]] as Grand Vizier. [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] took the Gulbadani name as his own when the party entered the house's service, tying his chosen identity to this lineage. Since the [[Raam|Inundation of Raam]] killed [[The Padshah]], the house is headless and its standing, and the name Tchk'Tchk carries, is in jeopardy.

**Status:** Headless since the Inundation of Raam; future uncertain

## Notes

The estate lies in the noble district, not the Templar district, and is now in ruin.

**Act II, Session 12:** [[Master Avariah]] believes House Gulbadan hid the truth about Raam's water. [[Babs]] recalled the family's tapestry mapping the city's aqueducts: every line led to a water source except the lines feeding the estate, which ran off the map. [[The Padshah]] had brushed this off as artistic licence. Avariah has asked the party to search the ruins for the secret. According to [[Neith]], beyond the grand hall where the party was raised into the household lay the family residence. Near where the manor joined the entablature (the raised servants' wing, now collapsed) is a room hidden by a false wall, leading to an area the Padshah visited seldom and always alone. Servants whispered of a wish-granting djinn or of dark rituals; Neith believes the place was solemn and sacred to the family.
