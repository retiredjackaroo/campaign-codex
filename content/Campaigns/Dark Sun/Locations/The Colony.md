# The Colony

![[The Colony.png]]

A sprawling [[Neogi]] complex in the deep northern reaches of [[The Cisterns]], beyond [[The Hidden Spring]]. Natural stone has been worked with black, chitin-like resin into ribs, lattices, webs and entire manufactured passages. A constant psionic chorus grows stronger toward the colony's centre and can inflict hive strain on intruders.

Known areas include a pantry of preserved dead, a holding pen for living thralls, a broodling-filled vent, a vast spiralling nursery lined with egg sacs, a psionically resonant chamber crossed by web bridges, and the chamber of [[The Hive Mother]].

In [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 9/Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]], the party located the nursery, destroyed [[The Voice]] in the resonant chamber and reached Mother herself. The confrontation remains unfinished.

In [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 10/Session 10 - Narrative|Session 10]], [[Derfel]] killed [[The Hive Mother]]. The hive began to wail and her chamber started to collapse, leaving the party to escape with three of its members unconscious. The fate of the nursery and the rest of the colony is unknown.

In [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 11/Session 11 - Narrative|Session 11]], the colony collapsed. Its resin structures had been psionically bound to Mother, and with her death they crumbled while the floors split and the ceilings came down. The party escaped through the vent into the old slave pen, which lies outside the colony's heart and held. The way back is sealed and considered certain death. Two unexplained sounds were heard during the escape: a persistent hiss like gas escaping under pressure, and later a roiling roar like a wave of force driven through a tight space.
