# The Hidden Spring

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/The Hidden Spring.png]]
A concealed [[The Alliance|Alliance]] sanctuary deep beneath [[Raam]], reached through [[The Cisterns]] and beyond a blank stretch of tunnel wall that splits open only under a preserver's psionic touch. Cloaked by illusion and psionic wards, it sits within a living aquifer: a cavern of vines and clear pooled water, lit by lazily drifting motes of magelight and tended with obvious care by a preserver's hand. Secret passages connect it to dry wells and forgotten cellars in the slum district, the means by which [[Leviath]] has moved safely above ground.

The party was led here by [[Leviath]] in Act II, Session 5 after crossing the aquifer lake and surviving the cloaker ambush along its ledge.

**Status:** Active; Alliance sanctuary
**City:** [[Raam]]
**Controlled by:** [[The Alliance]]
**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 5/Session 5 - Notes|Session 5 - Notes]]

## The cell

The sanctuary houses an Alliance cell of ten, four of them officers:

- [[Sarath]] – human preserver, cell leader
- [[Nisat of the Cavern Moss]] – half-elf druid of the spores
- [[Koresh Ironblood]] – mole warrior
- [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek "Cricket" Silvertwig]] – halfling ranger

## Notes

The cell lives by five aims (protect the Alliance, protect preservers, protect auxiliaries, oppose defilers, undermine the Sorcerer Kings); see [[The Alliance]] for detail. Alliance scouts checking unexplored reaches of the aquifer beyond the Spring have gone missing, an open thread at the close of Session 5.

**Session 11:** The party returned from [[The Colony]] as heroes and was offered an open invitation to join the cell. The sanctuary's grotto pool is where the cell receives [[Master Avariah]]'s dragonfly messengers. If the party goes ahead with the rescue of [[Korno]], the cell intends to abandon this safe house and leave signs for the party to find its new location.

**Session 12:** [[Master Avariah]] confirmed the cell will be relocated, since the party's knowledge of it would endanger it once they move against the Templars. The cell supplied each party member with healing potions brewed from local fungus and lichen, along with non-magical gear from its stores. A quiet elf, a cleric of the air, went up to the surface with the party. After the rescue, the party is to wait at the pool on the route that once led here. [[Salt]] remains below with the Alliance.
