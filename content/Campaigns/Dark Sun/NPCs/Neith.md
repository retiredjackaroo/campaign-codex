# Neith

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Neith.png]]

A half-giant, formerly lead enforcer and handmaiden to [[The Padshah]], head of the Gulbadan faction. The party freed her from captivity at the [[Gulbadan Estate]]. After the Inundation of Raam and the Padshah's death she returned to [[The Slums]], where she was born.

**Status:** Alive; a forced labourer in the slums (Act II, Session 12)
**Affiliation:** [[The Gulbadan Maulani]] (former)
**First appears:** <!-- link to episode once built -->

## Relationships

- [[The Padshah]]: served as lead enforcer and handmaiden; loved her, she says, more than perhaps anyone ever did
- [[Jalen Marek]]: helps keep him hidden; can get word to him
- [[Grainseed|Ammat Grainseed]]: works with her to guide people to Jalen

## Notable moments

- **Act II, Session 12:** Found half-starved in a tent city of forced labourers, worked in chain gangs and made to attend the cultists' morning sermons. Hushed [[Pterrence]] (by the beak) when he announced the party's plans aloud. Told the party of a room hidden behind a false wall near where the Gulbadan manor joined the collapsed entablature, a place the Padshah visited seldom and always alone. She dismissed servants' rumours of a wish-granting djinn or dark rituals, insisting the Padshah was ruthless but not dark and that whatever lay there was sacred to the family. She would not leave her fellow labourers to be punished for her absence, but took the party to [[Jalen Marek]].
