# The Voice

![[The Voice.png]]

A male [[The Templars|Templar]] found cocooned above a chasm in a psionically resonant chamber within [[The Colony]]. His exposed face was grey and atrophied. The party sensed that he was either the source of the chamber's hypnotic pulse or a conduit through which the hive spoke.

In [[Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]], [[Anselem]] and [[Derfel]] struck before he could speak, burning the cocoon with Fireball and flaming arrows. [[Pterrence]] caught his falling body with Levitate, and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] battered it with telekinetic force. The Voice warned that free will was a facade, called the party to join the hive's “continuation,” and used mass suggestion to compel Anselem and [[Kit-Chac]] toward [[The Hive Mother]].

[[Salt]] recovered [[The Voices Amulet|The Voice's Amulet]] before Pterrence released the charred body into the chasm.

**First appears:** [[Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]]
