# Nisat of the Cavern Moss

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Nisat of the Cavern Moss.png]]
A half-elf druid of the spores and one of the four officers of the [[The Alliance|Alliance]] cell at [[The Hidden Spring]]. Wiry, with sun-weathered skin, he carries a staff sprouting fresh green shoots. He wears green robes and deliberately smears his pale olive skin with algae, which he calls "anointing himself with the textures of the underworld." There is something otherworldly about him, as though he perceives truth beneath the surface. Pain does not disturb him; it is as natural to him as growth.

**Status:** Alive
**Race / role:** Half-elf; druid of the spores
**Affiliation:** [[The Alliance]] ([[The Hidden Spring]] cell)
**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 5/Session 5 - Notes|Session 5 - Notes]]

## Relationships

- [[Sarath]]: cell leader
- [[Koresh Ironblood]], [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek "Cricket" Silvertwig]]: fellow officers

## Notes

First introduced by [[Sarath]] alongside the other officers when the party arrived at the sanctuary in Act II, Session 5.

**Session 11:** Sent word to [[Master Avariah]] by plucking a shimmering green-and-blue dragonfly from the grotto pool, whispering to it and releasing it through the illusory wall. A strict follower of the aims, like [[Sarath]]. On Avariah's summons he said the aims are not always a straight path, and that she sees something in the deep places that ties back to the war above. [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] plans to draw on his knowledge of volatile foraged materials without telling him why.
