# Grave

[[Derfel]]'s fourth companion dog, following [[Dave]], [[Maeve]] and [[Brave]]. Derfel called him in the old slave pen beside [[The Colony]] immediately after the party escaped its collapse in Act II, Session 11. Pterrence, who had been unconscious when Brave died, noticed at once that the dog looked different.

**Status:** Alive\
**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 11/Session 11 - Narrative|Session 11 - Narrative]]

## Relationships

- [[Derfel]]: companion
- [[Brave]]: predecessor, killed in Act II, Session 10
