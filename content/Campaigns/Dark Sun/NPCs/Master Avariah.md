# Master Avariah

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Master Avariah.png]]
Leader of [[The Alliance]] (the Veiled Alliance) in [[Raam]], and the higher authority that [[Sarath]] and the [[The Hidden Spring|Hidden Spring]] cell answer to. She is seldom seen. She is tall and cloaked in flowing desert silks that shimmer like water in moonlight. She appears human but is older than she looks, perhaps with a trace of elven blood in ears that are large but not quite elven. Her eyes shine with an almost celestial light, with an intensity and sharpness beneath it. She carries a sceptre of bone wound in a spiral of obsidian, topped by a dark crystal whose inner firelight pulses like a heartbeat. She speaks softly and melodically, with an authority that never needs to rise, and she eats no meat.

**Status:** Met (Act II, Session 12)
**Affiliation:** [[The Alliance]]
**First heard:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 11/Session 11 - Notes|Session 11 - Notes]]
**First met:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 12/Session 12 - Notes|Session 12 - Notes]]

## Relationships

- [[Sarath]]: reports to her
- [[Leviath]]: long-standing friend of the Alliance; can reach Avariah without revealing her location
- [[Salt]]: singled out as "one of [this world's] rarest beings"; at her request he has stayed behind to help her
- [[Korno]]: she declined to back his rescue, judging his loyalty to the aims unproven

## Notes

**Session 11:** Her voice summoned the party through the Hidden Spring's grotto pool, via a dragonfly messenger.

**Session 12:** She met the party at a fungus-lit pool most of a day's journey deeper beneath Raam. She has studied fragments of the world before and holds stone tablets recovered from the vaults beneath the Ivory Palace. These tell of Rajaat, a great mortal who mastered pure, preserving magic when Athas was a paradise. She believes Raam's water does not dwindle as House Gulbadan claimed, and is fed by a tremendous source. She asked the party to search the [[Gulbadan Estate]] for the family's secret instead of risking themselves for Korno. If an everlasting source exists, she says she will release it so that no one controls it and the people need no longer serve the Templars. She recited the five aims as: to preserve life, to oppose defilers, to protect knowledge, to safeguard the hidden, and to train the next generation.

When the party refused to give up on Korno, she apologised for seeming to command them and set terms. They should do the estate (and the vault, if attempted) first, free Korno at night, then wait at the pool on the route to the Hidden Spring for her to make contact through Leviath. She will relocate the Hidden Spring cell because of what the party knows. She supplied healing potions and gear and pointed them to [[Neith]]. She then met Salt alone and asked him to stay. Salt judged her sincere in her belief, and far older than they had guessed. See [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 12/Session 12 - Notes|Session 12 - Notes]].
