# Master Avariah

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Master Avariah.png]]
The Alliance's overall leader, named for the first time in prep for Act II, Session 6, where the party is expected to raise the matter of [[Korno]] with them directly. Very likely the same higher, rarely-seen figure [[Sarath]] already answers to (see [[The Alliance]] and [[The Hidden Spring]]), who the Hidden Spring cell rumours to be close to Avangion transformation, though this identification is not yet confirmed in play.

**Status:** Heard but not yet met (Act II, Session 11)
**Affiliation:** [[The Alliance]]
**First heard:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 11/Session 11 - Notes|Session 11 - Notes]]

## Relationships

- [[Sarath]]: reports upward to this figure, per [[The Alliance]]
- [[Korno]]: subject of the party's expected appeal to Avariah for help freeing him

## Notes

**Unconfirmed page.** Created ahead of play from prep notes only, title, gender, race and manner of appearance are all unknown at this point. A portrait has since been added from the Roll20 journal's own "Master Avariah" handout, so the art is confirmed even though the in-fiction reveal is not; the party has still not actually met her, and Session 6 ended up deferring the matter again in favour of the scout search. Once she's actually met in play, confirm whether Avariah is indeed Sarath's superior referenced in Session 5, update this page with what's actually learned, flip `dg-publish`/`publish` to true, and update the cross-references in [[The Alliance]] and [[The Hidden Spring]] (both currently describe the figure only anonymously). If Avariah turns out to be a different person entirely, flag it and I'll split this out properly.

**Session 11:** Sarath sent word of [[Korno]]'s fate and the party's plan by dragonfly messenger. The next evening the dragonfly returned, and a woman's voice, measured and solemn with heartache beneath its poise, spoke through the stone and water of the grotto: "Leviath, you will bring them to me. The strangers who walk beside you are to be known to me in full. Make no delay. The place will be prepared." To the cell her word is law. This confirms Avariah as the figure Sarath answers to and establishes that she is a woman; she gave no answer on the rescue itself. The cell's officers note that under her direction their focus has shifted from striking defilers to charting the Underdark.
