# Jalen Marek

![[Jalen Marek.png]]

One of [[Korno]]'s lieutenants, and his last remaining captain. Lean and scarred, sharp-eyed despite the exhaustion that weighs on him, with a voice hoarse from too much dust and too little water. He leads what is left of the slums resistance from a hidden safe house in [[The Slums]], with about half a dozen hardened fighters, and the slum-dwellers keep him hidden.

**Status:** Alive; in hiding  
**Affiliation:** Korno's slums resistance  
**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 12/Session 12 - Narrative|Session 12 - Narrative]]

## Relationships

- [[Korno]]: his leader; Jalen has watched him on the Templar gate and wants him freed, or killed rather than sacrificed
- [[Neith]]: vouched for the party and brought them to him
- [[Grainseed|Ammat Grainseed]]: guides visitors to the safe house; with Neith, carries word to Jalen

## Notable moments

- **Act II, Session 12:** Named by [[Leviath]] as the new leader of the slums resistance and met in person through Neith. He had thought his people were all that was left. His heart says to free Korno, but his mind fears they are too few. If Korno cannot be broken loose, he would rather end him than let him be made a puppet of the cult of Dregoth or of Jahandar. He agreed to stage a diversion outside the Templar walls to draw off guards. His fighters will be ready from midnight the following night and can be in position within an hour of word reaching them. He also explained the Voice of Dregoth and Restoration Day to the party. See [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 12/Session 12 - Notes|Session 12 - Notes]].
