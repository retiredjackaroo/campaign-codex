# The Hive Mother

![[The Hive Mother.png]]

The enormous mother of the [[Neogi]] colony beneath [[The Hidden Spring]]. Her bloated body lies in a chamber shaped like the belly of a beast, attended by adult Neogi and a [[Hook Horror]], with broodlings crawling across her and egg sacs surrounding the room.

Mother speaks directly into the minds of creatures throughout [[The Colony]]. Her voice can feel warm, calm and maternal, offering an end to thought and choice. In battle she has demonstrated psychic domination, fear, mass compulsion and a mind lash, while her attendants punish resistance and restrain targets with psychic hooks.

In [[Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]], [[Kit-Chac]] reached her chamber alone under a mass suggestion. He resisted her attempt to dominate him and attacked. The party arrived two rounds later; [[Derfel]] and [[Anselem]] wounded Mother, but she retained concentration on her effects. The session ended with Mother alive and the fight unfinished.

**First appears:** [[Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]]
