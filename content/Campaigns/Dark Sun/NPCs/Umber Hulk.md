# Umber Hulk

An umber hulk ambushed the party with two [[Neogi]] in the northern aquifer tunnels during the search for [[Narek]] and [[Scylla]]. Cocoon-like silk remains were found in the same chamber.

A second Umber Hulk guarded living prisoners in the Neogi holding pen during [[Session 7 - Narrative|Session 7]]. In [[Session 8 - Narrative|Session 8]], it attempted to burrow away but [[Derfel]] killed it through the narrow opening with an arrow and Hail of Thorns.

**Status:** Two defeated
**First appears:** [[Session 6 - Narrative|Session 6]]
