# Umber Hulk

An umber hulk ambushed the party with two [[Neogi]] in the northern aquifer tunnels during the search for [[Narek]] and [[Scylla]]. Cocoon-like silk remains were found in the same chamber.

A second Umber Hulk guarded living prisoners in the Neogi holding pen during [[Session 7 - Narrative|Session 7]]. It remained alive and wounded when the session ended mid-battle.

**Status:** One defeated; one active
