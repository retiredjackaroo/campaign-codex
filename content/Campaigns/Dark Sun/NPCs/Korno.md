# Korno

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Pasted image 20260628010528.png]]
Head of the Slums faction. Husband of [[Leviath]]. Captured by [[The Templars]] in Act II, Session 4 when his safe house in [[The Slums]] was overrun by a combined Templar, Noble, and Dregoth force. The party arrived too late to warn him.

**Status:** Captured (taken by Templars, Act II, Session 4)
**First appears:** [[Session 4 - Notes]]

## Relationships

- [[Leviath]]: wife

## Notes

His rescue remains unresolved. In Act II, Session 5 the party deferred it again while sheltering at [[The Hidden Spring]]; [[Koresh Ironblood]] implied that helping the Alliance with its own problems first would go a long way toward the cell agreeing to help free him. In Session 6 the party deferred again, this time to search for two missing Alliance scouts; [[Leviath]] continues to report no news beyond his having been taken into the Temple district.
