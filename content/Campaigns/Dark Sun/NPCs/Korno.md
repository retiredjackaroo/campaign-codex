# Korno

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Pasted image 20260628010528.png]]
Head of the Slums faction and an ally of [[Leviath]]. Captured by [[The Templars]] in Act II, Session 4 when his safe house in [[The Slums]] was overrun by a combined Templar, Noble, and Dregoth force. The party arrived too late to warn him.

**Status:** Alive; held by the Templars and marked for sacrifice on Restoration Day (Act II, Session 12)
**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 4/Session 4 - Notes|Session 4 - Notes]]

## Relationships

- [[Leviath]]: ally
- [[Jalen Marek]]: lieutenant; now leads what remains of his resistance

## Notes

His rescue remains unresolved. In Act II, Session 5 the party deferred it again while sheltering at [[The Hidden Spring]]; [[Koresh Ironblood]] implied that helping the Alliance with its own problems first would go a long way toward the cell agreeing to help free him. In Session 6 the party deferred again, this time to search for two missing Alliance scouts; [[Leviath]] continues to report no news beyond his having been taken into the Temple district.

**Session 11:** [[Leviath]] brought word that Korno is alive. [[The Templars]] parade him daily as a trophy, chaining him to the wall above the main gate to the Templar district, on the thoroughfare between [[The Elven Market]] and the trade districts, and take him back inside each evening. A holy day has been declared, the first festival of Dregoth, and on it Korno and other prisoners are to be sacrificed as the city's first offering to its new god; the date is unknown. The party agreed to try to free him, and is planning a diversion with explosives and Fog Cloud to snatch him from the gate. See [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 11/Session 11 - Notes|Session 11 - Notes]].

**Session 12:** [[Master Avariah]] declined to back his rescue, judging his path not that of the Alliance's aims and his loyalty to them unproven. She asked the party to free him at night from wherever he is held inside the Templar district, rather than from the gate by day. Rumour on the street, confirmed by [[Jalen Marek]], is that he looks healthy and restored every morning, as though he is being kept strong for the sacrifice. That sacrifice is to fall on Restoration Day, once the "Voice of Dregoth" has spoken to the city. Jalen will stage a diversion for the rescue, but would rather see Korno dead than offered to the cult. See [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 12/Session 12 - Notes|Session 12 - Notes]].
