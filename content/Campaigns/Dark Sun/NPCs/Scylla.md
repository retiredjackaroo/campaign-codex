# Scylla

**Status:** Missing

An elven female scout attached to [[The Alliance]]'s [[The Hidden Spring]] cell. Sent north into unmapped reaches of the aquifer alongside fellow scout Narek to investigate a psionic disturbance, per orders passed down through Master Avariah. The pair missed two consecutive check-ins, prompting [[Sarath]] to send [[Derfel]], [[Her]], [[Pterrence]], [[Anselem]], [[Salt]], [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] and [[Koresh Ironblood]] to search for them ([[Session 6 - Narrative|Session 6]]).

A scrap of Scylla's torn clothing was found snagged on rock in the tunnels she and Narek passed through. [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] later confirmed she wore a ceramic nose piercing, offered as an identifying detail should [[Pterrence]] get the chance to cast Locate Object. Given the [[Umber Hulk]] and [[Neogi]] ambush, and the cocoon-like silk remains found in the same chamber, her fate is uncertain and her survival in serious doubt.
