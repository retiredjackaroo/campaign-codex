# Grainseed

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Grainseed.png]]

Ammat Grainseed, a young elven boy from [[The Slums]] with pointed ears, smudged cheeks and clothes discoloured as much by age as by dirt. He speaks telepathically, briefly and directly. He first reached out to the party with his telepathy when they came to the slums to meet [[Korno]].
