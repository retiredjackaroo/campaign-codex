# Sarath

A human preserver and the leader of the [[The Alliance|Alliance]] cell based at [[The Hidden Spring]]. She appears to be in her late thirties or early forties, her robes marked with intricate green stitching in the shape of an unfurling leaf, her hair pulled into a braid adorned with tiny bone beads. She greeted the party and [[Leviath]]'s column of refugees into the sanctuary in Act II, Session 5 with the words "By the winds, it's been too long." Serious without being severe, thoughtful without being cold, she carries the weight of her cell's safety and silence, and is always present but rarely joins in the easy camaraderie of the other officers.

Sarath answers to someone higher in the Alliance whom even her own officers almost never see, contacted only by animal messengers or rare appearances. In an unguarded moment it was suggested that this figure may be close to becoming an [[The Alliance|Avangion]], a celestial counter to the Sorcerer Kings' draconic ambition.

**Status:** Alive
**Race / role:** Human; preserver, cell leader
**Affiliation:** [[The Alliance]] ([[The Hidden Spring]] cell)
**First appears:** [[Session 5 - Notes]]

## Relationships

- [[Leviath]]: greeted as an old friend; provides sanctuary to her and the refugees she leads
- [[Nisat of the Cavern Moss]], [[Koresh Ironblood]], [[Tarek "Cricket" Silvertwig]]: her three fellow officers of the cell
- Unseen Alliance leadership: answers to a higher figure rumoured to be near Avangion transformation
