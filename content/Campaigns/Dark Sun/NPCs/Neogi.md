# Neogi

![[Neogi.png]]

Two neogi fought the party alongside an [[Umber Hulk]] in the northern aquifer tunnels. Their hive-mind briefly charmed [[Babs|Her]] and turned her against [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]]. Her recovered strike beheaded the second neogi.

In [[Session 7 - Narrative|Session 7]], the party entered a Neogi pantry and holding complex. A taskmaster used enthralled humanoids as shields before [[Derfel]] killed it. Four more Neogi guarded living prisoners with a second Umber Hulk; one was killed by [[Koresh Ironblood]], while at least three remained when play stopped. Their chorus inflicted hive strain across the party and left [[Anselem]] enthralled.

In [[Session 8 - Narrative|Session 8]], the remaining holding-pen Neogi were destroyed by Tchk'Tchk's Shatter, Babs's blade and Anselem's Radiance of the Dawn. The party rescued all eight prisoners, then discovered and burned a vent carpeted with thousands of pale hatchlings. The deeper colony and the entity called Mother remain active.

**First appears:** [[Session 6 - Narrative|Session 6]]
