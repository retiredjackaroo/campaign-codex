# Neogi

![[Neogi.png]]

Two neogi fought the party alongside an [[Umber Hulk]] in the northern aquifer tunnels. Their hive-mind briefly charmed [[Babs|Her]] and turned her against [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]]. Her recovered strike beheaded the second neogi.

In [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 7/Session 7 - Narrative|Session 7]], the party entered a Neogi pantry and holding complex. A taskmaster used enthralled humanoids as shields before [[Derfel]] killed it. Four more Neogi guarded living prisoners with a second Umber Hulk; one was killed by [[Koresh Ironblood]], while at least three remained when play stopped. Their chorus inflicted hive strain across the party and left [[Anselem]] enthralled.

In [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 8/Session 8 - Narrative|Session 8]], the remaining holding-pen Neogi were destroyed by Tchk'Tchk's Shatter, Babs's blade and Anselem's Radiance of the Dawn. The party rescued all eight prisoners, then discovered and burned a vent carpeted with thousands of pale hatchlings. The deeper colony and the entity called Mother remain active.

In [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 9/Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]], the party found a vast nursery guarded by about a dozen adults, destroyed the cocooned Templar conduit called [[The Voice]], and fought two Neogi alongside a [[Hook Horror]]. [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] hurled both Neogi into a chasm. Two further attendants defend [[The Hive Mother]] in her chamber, where the battle remains unfinished.

In [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 10/Session 10 - Narrative|Session 10]], Mother's attendants fought under her legendary commands; they dropped [[Babs]] with venomous bites and one carried her off before [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk]] teleported her free. Four egg sacs burst into swarms of broodlings that tried to burrow into the party's flesh and left [[Brave]] badly wounded. A rockfall crushed one attendant and trapped another. [[The Hive Mother]] was killed, and the hive began to wail as her chamber collapsed.

**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 6/Session 6 - Narrative|Session 6]]
