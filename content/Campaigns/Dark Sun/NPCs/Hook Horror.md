# Hook Horror

Large subterranean creatures with hooked forelimbs and clacking, beak-like mouths. The [[Neogi]] use them as servants and guards within [[The Colony]].

In [[Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]], one hook horror entered the chamber of [[The Voice]] alongside two Neogi. [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] stopped it with Command, then hurled it into the chamber's bottomless chasm with Telekinetic Propel.

A further hook horror guards [[The Hive Mother]]. It remained alive but wounded when Session 9 ended.

**First appears:** [[Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]]

## Notes

The table frequently pronounced the creature's name as “Hukara.”
