# Narek

**Status:** Missing

A human male scout attached to [[The Alliance]]'s [[The Hidden Spring]] cell. Sent north into unmapped reaches of the aquifer alongside fellow scout Scylla to investigate a psionic disturbance, per orders passed down through Master Avariah. The pair missed two consecutive check-ins, prompting [[Sarath]] to send [[Derfel]], [[Her]], [[Pterrence]], [[Anselem]], [[Salt]], [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] and [[Koresh Ironblood]] to search for them ([[Session 6 - Narrative|Session 6]]).

Narek was not encountered directly, and no trace of him specifically has yet been found. Given the [[Umber Hulk]] and [[Neogi]] ambush, and the cocoon-like silk remains found in the same chamber, his fate is uncertain and his survival in serious doubt.
