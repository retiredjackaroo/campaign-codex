# Narek

**Status:** Rescued; hive-conditioned

A human male scout attached to [[The Alliance]]'s [[The Hidden Spring]] cell. Sent north into unmapped reaches of the aquifer alongside fellow scout Scylla to investigate a psionic disturbance, per orders passed down through Master Avariah. The pair missed two consecutive check-ins, prompting [[Sarath]] to send [[Derfel]], [[Babs|Her]], [[Pterrence]], [[Anselem]], [[Salt]], [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] and [[Koresh Ironblood]] to search for them ([[Session 6 - Narrative|Session 6]]).

Narek was not encountered directly, and no trace of him specifically has yet been found. Given the [[Umber Hulk]] and [[Neogi]] ambush, and the cocoon-like silk remains found in the same chamber, his fate is uncertain and his survival in serious doubt.

**Session 7:** The party opened the pantry's cocoons and found many preserved dead, then followed possible ceramic nose-jewellery signals to a Neogi holding pen containing living prisoners. Narek was not positively identified in either group and remains missing.

**Session 8:** Found alive among eight prisoners in the Neogi holding pen. Narek and the others were physically capable but deeply conditioned by the hive, repeatedly referring to Mother and asking whether they were being transferred. [[Salt]] convinced them that Mother had ordered their relocation and led them out without casualties. Narek is rescued, but his mental condition remains unresolved.
