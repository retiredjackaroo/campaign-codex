## Where We Are Now

Deep beneath [[The Hidden Spring]], [[The Hive Mother]] is dead. [[Derfel]] put an arrow through her eye moments after a rockfall killed his companion [[Brave]], and her death has set the whole hive wailing. Her chamber in [[The Colony]] is coming down around the party.

![[The Hive Mother.png]]
_The Hive Mother, killed at the end of Session 10._

Derfel is the only one still standing. [[Babs]] is unconscious but stable; [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]] and [[Pterrence]] are unconscious and not yet stabilised; [[Salt]] is pinned under rubble with broodlings burrowing into him. [[Anselem]] has walked out of the lair under Mother's mass suggestion, and [[Kit-Chac]]'s whereabouts were not established. Next comes the escape, carrying three unconscious friends through a collapsing lair.

![[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Assets/Pasted image 20260627123731.png]]
_Brave, Derfel's third companion, killed in Session 10._
