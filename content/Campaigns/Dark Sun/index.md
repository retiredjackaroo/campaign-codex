## Where We Are Now

The party made it out. With [[The Hive Mother]] dead, [[The Colony]] collapsed around them, and they escaped through the vent carrying [[Babs]], [[Pterrence]] and [[Tchk Tchk Gulbadani|Tchk'Tchk Gulbadani]], all of whom [[Anselem]] then revived with an improvised poultice. [[Derfel]] has a new companion, [[Grave]].

![[Session 11 - The Hive Collapses.png]]
_The colony comes apart after Mother's death._

Back at [[The Hidden Spring]], the party is now welcomed almost as members of [[The Alliance]]. [[Leviath]] reports that [[The Templars]] hold [[Raam]] absolutely, the masked clerics of [[Dregoth Cultists|Dregoth]] lead a new state religion, and [[Korno]] is alive: he is displayed daily above a gate to the Templar district and is to be sacrificed at the first festival of Dregoth. The party has agreed to free him, and [[Tarek Cricket Silvertwig|Tarek]] is quietly gathering explosives for a distraction.

[[Sarath]] judged the rescue beyond her authority and sent word to [[Master Avariah]]. Avariah's answer came through the grotto pool: Leviath is to bring the party to her. Next comes that meeting.
