## Where We Are Now

Deep beneath [[Raam]], the party finally met [[Master Avariah]]. She believes House Gulbadan hid the truth about the city's water: that it is fed by a tremendous, undying source. She asked the party to search the ruins of the [[Gulbadan Estate]] for that secret instead of risking themselves for [[Korno]]. They would not abandon him. In the end she agreed terms: the estate first, then Korno, freed by night, and afterwards they are to wait underground for her word. [[Salt]] stayed behind at her request.

![[Session 12 - The Masters Pool.webp]]
_The Master's pool, deep beneath Raam._

Back on the surface, Raam is quiet with exhaustion, its streets watched by Templar patrols and dotted with shrines to [[Dregoth Cultists|Dregoth]]. Korno is to be sacrificed on Restoration Day, once the mysterious Voice of Dregoth has spoken. In the slums the party found [[Neith]], who told them of a hidden room in the Gulbadan manor. She led them to [[Jalen Marek]], Korno's last captain, whose fighters will stage a diversion from midnight tomorrow. First the party needs fresh papers from the Nightrunners. That ends Act II; Act III begins next session.
