# The Voices Amulet

A ceramic amulet recovered from [[The Voice]] by [[Salt]] using Mage Hand in [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 9/Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]]. Its insignia identifies its former wearer as a [[The Templars|Templar]] responsible for a garment or tannery district or portfolio in [[Raam]].

The amulet was scorched when [[Anselem]] burned the Voice's cocoon. Its properties were identified in Act II, Session 11: it requires attunement and acts as an amulet of the devout +2, granting +2 to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs, and once per day it lets its wearer use Channel Divinity without expending a use. Its insignia is that of the Templar order overseeing Raam's tanneries and dyeing industries.

**Current holder:** [[Anselem]] (attuned, Act II, Session 11); previously carried for the party after [[Salt]] recovered it
**First appears:** [[Campaigns/Dark Sun/Sessions/Act II/Session 9/Session 9 - Narrative|Session 9]]
