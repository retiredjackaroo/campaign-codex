# The Skull

![[Pasted image 20260628014120.png]]
A skull recovered from the remains of the sand elemental defeated in [[The Cisterns]] during Act II, Session 4. The skull was originally thrown into the aqueduct by [[Dregoth Cultists]] after they detected the party descending through the outer well. It animated into a huge sand elemental, pulling stone and sand into itself over time: the longer the party had taken to descend, the larger it would have grown.

On the elemental's defeat the skull fell free from the collapsing mass. [[Her]] moved in and took it from the wreckage.

**Status:** Destroyed; a single fragment retained by [[Pterrence]] (Act II, Session 5)\
**Origin:** Dregoth Cultists\
**First appears:** [[Session 4 - Notes]]

## Notes

Significance unknown. The fact that the Dregoth Cultists used it as a weapon suggests it may be a reusable or repurposable tool of elemental summoning. Worth investigating.

In Act II, Session 5 [[Pterrence]] identified the skull as the still-active anchor of an elemental transmutation ritual: left alone it would begin drawing sand and stone back into itself within a day and reform. The party destroyed it rather than risk that. [[Her]] battered it, [[Kit-Chac]] cracked it with his gythka, and Her finished it with an airborne longsword strike, shattering it across the tunnel floor. [[Pterrence]] threaded a single surviving fragment onto a cord to see whether it shows any sign of reactivating once the day is up.
